## 6. UNKNOWNs and limits

- **Size is reported, not matched.** The BmAK side gives systems/emitters/lifetime/size in
  UE units; the JC3 side gives effect rows/arrays/bytes. No unit or scaling bridge exists
  between them, so the ranking is kind -> donor -> fleet use, and the size numbers stand
  beside the choice for a human to review. A size-fit claim would be `UNKNOWN`.
- **No in-game proof.** Nothing here is observed in a running game (R6: no launch before
  phase V). Every `verify` cell is a Q probe that can fail.
- **Which weapon ids FX-2 rides on is T-11's** (D-3/D-7 decide slots and lethality). The
  effects named are the fleet's own for that weapon *kind*, measured per id: the weapon
  bundles carry them, so a clone brings them (w619 16, w610 14, w618 12, w608 5 effects;
  w602/w603/w604 ship no bundle of their own, G2-10).
- **`EngineNitro` is a usage name, not a proven boost trigger.** G2-22/23 read the hash and
  the attachments; that the flame appears on the boost input is a Q-1/Q-2 probe.
- **The `.effc` writer is proved for in-place patches, not for growth.** `effc_writer.py
  check` re-encodes 95/95 sampled shipped effects byte-for-byte (91.49% of their bytes from
  decoded fields, 0 unexplained bytes) and writes one edited copy
  (`EmitterTemplates[0].MaxParticles` 60 -> 317) that re-reads; adding or removing array
  elements is expressible (explicit u64 offsets and counts) but was not built or re-read, so
  T-FX12/T-FX16 carry it as the gap card `B-17` closes (`effc-format-writable.md` §4/§6).
