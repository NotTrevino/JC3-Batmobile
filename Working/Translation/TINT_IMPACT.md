### 2.1 The two palette rows, read closely

* **T-MA4** asks for `0.45` "in every paint block and in all 10 palette tints" and is
  `dispo: keep` with `why: build carries the proposal`. The palette half is already
  satisfied by the donor file: all ten records decode at gloss `0.45` before the build
  copies it, so no palette byte is written and nothing needs to be. The paint-block half
  is an RBM material edit, a different carrier (not judged here).
* **T-MA7** claims "10 tints, all `specular_gloss` 0.45, `clear_coat` 1, `metallic` 1
  (read from the shipped donor module in the build)". Decoded from the RC2 palette: 10
  records, every one gloss `0.45`, metallic `1.0`, clear_coat `1.0`. The row's
  "leave the palette untouched" is exactly what the build did (same sha256 as bm-005).
