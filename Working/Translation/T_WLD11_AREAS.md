## 6. The two oracles

### O-1 - re-indexing onto existing deform points

A vertex may be re-pointed at index `i` only if that point already
(a) sits outside the Batmobile shell, (b) inside the donor band, (c) on a standing face,
and (d) within 0.3 m of the vertex it replaces -
otherwise the area's shape changes.

Pool of points that pass (a)-(c) on the Batmobile: **15 of 135**.

| area | vertices with a legal substitute (itself or a neighbour) | of | complete |
|---|---:|---:|:-:|
| `front_area` | 0 | 6 | **no** |
| `front_signature_area` | 0 | 6 | **no** |
| `main_area` | 0 | 10 | **no** |

**O-1 fails.** 0/3 areas could be rebuilt; the pool of points that pass on the Batmobile is 15 of 135 and the highest donor point (y 1.5242 m) is below the Batmobile roof surface (y 2.0811 m) and deck (y 1.3612 m) that the areas must cover.
A standing surface on the Batmobile roof cannot be built out of donor points: the
highest donor point is below the roof the area is supposed to cover, so no re-indexing
can lift `main_area` onto it. Re-indexing is therefore **not supported by the data**.

### O-2 - moving the area onto the shell

The cache invariant holds to **0.0 m** on every vertex, so a
`vertices3D_local`-only edit would leave the module contradicting itself, and any honest
move is an edit of the 22 `DeformPoints.start_transform` values.

- the 22 points' part bindings resolve on the Batmobile part table (indices in B-04's kept range): **True**;
- 6 positions are shared by two areas, so a move must move both copies together;
- the move target itself is computable (ray-cast onto the body mesh, as T-13 did for
  the `point.stunt*` nodes), but **which field the runtime trusts** - the stored
  `vertices3D_local` or a recomputation from `DeformPoints` - is not decoded, and
  whether the deform list is also read by the deformation/skinning system is not
  decoded either. A value written blind would be a guess, so this half stays UNKNOWN.

## 7. UNKNOWNs

- **The runtime precedence** between `vertices3D_local` and the bound `DeformPoints`
  positions. Nothing in the entity, the geom sidecar or the build's modules states it.
- **Whether the areas are validated at runtime** against the deform list (a mismatch
  between the two fields is exactly the state a move would create).
- **Whether `DeformPoints` is also consumed by deformation/skinning**; if it is, moving
  a point moves geometry, not just a standing plane.
- **The donor band's upper edge is a sample of 22 points**, not a per-vertex guarantee: it
  is the tightest evidence available from the files and is used as such.
