## 6. What the files do not give (UNKNOWN)

1. **No lock-on time or range on the JC3 side.** The `AutoAim` block is sticky aim (angles and strength per shooter context): 132 member names on `w609_fighterjet_missile_air_lockon`, of which 0 contain 'lock'. BmAK's `MissileLockOnDuration` and `LockOnRange` are therefore reported, not written (T-WP3/T-WP4).
2. **No data-side disable state for the Immobilizer.** `CEMPWeapon` w702/w503 exist but carry no EMP field, the pulse tune has no duration, target class or disable member (G2-16), and `is_disabled_by_emp` (exe 0x24EA080) has 0 asset bindings: the writer is B-18's probe build, not a value.
3. **No trigger for the entity pulse.** 14 of the fleet's 307 `CForcePulse` objects have no owner pointer and the firing moment is UNKNOWN (G2-17): B-18 (EMP) and B-20 (sonar) author the trigger; no file supplies one.
4. **No damage ratio for anything the Vulcan shoots.** BmAK gives no soldier, APC or helicopter health, so WPN-2's damage is the clone's shipped number; only the tank ratio (1200/50) exists and it belongs to T-14.
5. **No soldier target number.** Q-3 #3 asks for shots-to-kill a soldier; no file on either side has a soldier health value, so that half of the take is a recording, not a prediction.
6. **`InaccuracyVsTime` points are not decoded** (the struct is 274 B in `WEAPONS.json` with `named_by` but no point list). WPN-2's spread row keeps JC3's numbers and records the shape from G1-23.
7. **JC3's `velocity` unit is not stated** in any decoded file. The /100 bridge is `derived` and the Q-3 flight-time take is what can fail it.
8. **The tracks and textures of the muzzle flashes** are T-17's rows; this card only names the effect each weapon rides.
