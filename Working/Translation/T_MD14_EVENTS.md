## 3. Why those three JC3 events

| family | BmAK names | why it is the nearest decoded JC3 function |
|---|---|---|
| engine | 1: `Play_BattlemodeEngine` | engine bed |
| deploy | 13: `Play_BattleMode_In_A` … `Play_BattleMode_InRev_F` | the only decoded mechanical open on a vehicle |
| retract | 16: `Play_BattleMode_Out_A` … `Play_BattleMode_OutRev_G` | the only decoded mechanical close on a vehicle |

The functions are the ones the brief names: the engine bed is *engine*; the In/Out
layers are mechanical transitions, and the only decoded mechanical events a donor
vehicle module fires are the door open/close pair.  There is no decoded *boost*,
*horn* or *impact* edge for a mode change: the boost event
(`exhaust_upgrade_nitrous`) is a nitro layer, the horn ships on no Batmobile event, and
the impact events are damage-break roles.  So those three are not used here.
