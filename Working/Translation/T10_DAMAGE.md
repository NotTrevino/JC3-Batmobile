The card asks for a predicted table in which small arms show no visible loss and heavy
weapons sit within ±20 % of the BmAK ratio. The three candidate health models do not
agree, so the status is model-conditional and is printed here rather than hidden:

| Done clause | status |

`--check` prints the same three lines and fails if a failing cell has no pending probe,
or if a probe is listed for a cell that passes.

## 7. UNKNOWNs and limits

- **The health scale is a switch, not a translation.** 36000 is 9x the fleet maximum of
  4000. It is chosen because the card's criterion is the BmAK ratio and no in-domain value
  can reach it (4000 gives 3 hits under H0, 6 under H1, 3 under H2). If B decides the
  fleet domain is a hard ceiling, ARM-2's ratio becomes a named omission and `max_health`
  returns to 4000.
- **The models disagree about small arms** (H1: 0.3 damage per pistol round; H2: nothing;
  H0: 16.667 % of the pool per 200 rounds). H1/H2 are inside ARM-1; H0 is not, and H0 is
  only reachable if the armour members are ignored, which the fleet census says they are
  not on any armoured vehicle.
- **Option A's gate scope is UNKNOWN for a vehicle root.** The flag is decoded as an
  int32 (`epe+1167`) with the game's own value; whether a `CCar` root honours it is not
  proven (`DAMAGE_MECHANISMS.md` section 4). The card's choice does not depend on it:
  option B is the only option whose numbers come from decoded fields.
- **`bullet_heavy` is unused** because no G2-11 weapon component is decoded into that
  channel; if one is, it is the cheaper way to ARM-1 and this row should be revisited.
- **Wheels are at the ceiling, not proven unbreakable** (section 2).
- **ARM-5 and ARM-6 are named omissions with owners**, not gaps this card can close.
