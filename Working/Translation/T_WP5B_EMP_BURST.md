## 6. Binding it to the Batmobile's button

JC3's input layer already reaches a use-item action: `INPUT.md` records the immobilizer / USE_ITEM path as **reachable** on the `B` track. So the binding does not need a new button - it needs an action behind the existing one. Three concrete bindings, cheapest first:

1. **Weapon slot** - clone the donor's second driver slot and point it at a `CEMPWeapon` entity (route b). Fires on the existing driver-weapon button. Status: derived, unproven.
2. **Use-item graph** - the truck's `S_STUNT_USE_ITEM_VEHICLE` graph on the car (route c). Fires on the contextual use button, i.e. when the car is stationary or slow, not on demand. Status: derived.
3. **Pulse on detonation** - only a blast, no button. Status: decoded that it works as a blast, useless as an EMP.

## 8. COMPROMISES - each with the reason it exists

**1. The EMP burst ships as a blast, if it ships at all.**

- What is lost: the disable. Every decoded carrier (the pulse, the tune, the truck's graph) fires a damage blast; the disable itself has no member anywhere in either game's decoded data.
- Why no better route was found: three probes are named above ((b) `CEMPWeapon` in a vehicle slot, (c) the use-item graph, (d) a graph script) and none of them has been run - the test ban forbids firing a weapon in-game, and the graph bytecode is undecoded.
- RECOMMENDED COMPROMISE: ship the `CForcePulse` + `truck_emp.ftune` copy on the car centre, tuned to BmAK's 75 m intent by scaling the pulse radius, and label it in-game as a blast until probe (b) returns. Accuracy lost: the car cannot stop a pursuing vehicle, which is the entire purpose of the gadget; the 2/4/8/12 s ladder is lost with it.

**2. One power level instead of four.**

- What is lost: charge-to-level, its 0/0.5/1.0/1.5 s ladder, the four discharge effects and the four audio switches.
- Why: JC3's pulse carries one radius and one tune; there is no per-level member on any carrier.
- RECOMMENDED COMPROMISE: bind the single pulse to the tap detent and drop the hold detent, so the control at least matches BmAK's tap. Accuracy lost: the player cannot choose a longer disable; the hold input and the four-stage visual build-up are gone.

**3. No wheel-EMP material flash.**

- What is lost: the wheels lighting up (`Batmobile_V3_Wheel_EMP_MAT_INST` on material slot 0) while the burst is charged.
- Why: JC3's per-vehicle material slots are decoded, but no decoded member swaps a material on a timer or a state.
- RECOMMENDED COMPROMISE: emit the discharge effect only and skip the wheel material. Accuracy lost: the visible tell that the gadget is charging.
