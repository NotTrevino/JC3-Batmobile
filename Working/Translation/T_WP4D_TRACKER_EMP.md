### 0.1 Why each change, in one line of evidence

- **C1** - no decoded record carries it. 2464 is the offset of the inherited field only inside the `RPursuitModeMissileLauncher` chain (classes: `RBatmobileEMPMissile`, `RBatmobileVehicleTracker`, `RPursuitModeMissileLauncher`); among every export of that lineage in the four packages only the 1 `Batmobile_EMP_Missile_Launcher` tag(s) serialize it, and no `Vehicle_Tracker` does; the class default object has no tag at 2464 either. The 3 other tags at that offset in the base package belong to classes outside the chain and are a different field at the same number.
- **C2** - both launcher payload pointers resolve to `Batmobile_EMPMissile_Projectile_ForCars` (export 5116); the sibling (export 16) is referenced by 0 export(s) in `Playable_Batmobile_SF.upk`. The fired archetype serializes no AkEvent at all.
- **C3** - `Batmobile_EMPMissile_Projectile_ForCars` serializes 10 tags, 0 of them scalar and 0 of them an AkEvent; the 60.0 sits on the sibling, which no launcher points at.
- **C4** - the two rows of that table resolve to two different exports.
- **C5** - 5461 * 360 / 65536 = 29.9981689..., not exactly 30; the sign convention (which way +pitch tilts the socket) is not in any decoded file.
