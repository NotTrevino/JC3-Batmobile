## 6. Answer in one line each

* **thrust** is readable: 3500 cu additive cap, 600 cu/s² accel, 2000 cu/s² decel and the 7-point torque curve. The cap and accel reach JC3 through
  T-B2, the curve through T-B1 (as one constant); **decel has no row**.
* **duration** is readable (2 s, `BoostDuration` @664; donut 6 s), but it
  is not a JC3 field: it appears only as a term of T-B2's min(). **No row translates duration**;
  JC3's 4/8 s nitro use-time is a donor default with no row.
* **recharge** is readable (12 s, `BoostCooldown` InlineArray 3, tier 0) and has
  **no row**; neither does `BoostCooldownUpgradeLevel`. JC3's refill is its own default.
* **speed cap**: the boost-additive cap 3500 cu (126 km/h) is covered by T-B2;
  the base `SVehicle.MaxSpeed` 4000 cu (144 km/h) has no row, and the
  336.4 km/h target is T-E5's assumed wiki figure.
