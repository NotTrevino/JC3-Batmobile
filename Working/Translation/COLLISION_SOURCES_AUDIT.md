## 3. What the audit found, in one paragraph

The ledger's collision section never quotes the drivable car's geometry. Its box figures and
counts come from `Batmobile_V3_Destroyed_Physics` - the asset `PHYSICS.md` explicitly says is
handed to the **destroyed** wreck and has **no chassis body** - while the drivable car's own
decoded geometry (`Batmobile_V3_Physics` 3 boxes; the 43 + 13 convex elements of the
mode-switched collision meshes) appears in none of the five rows or in `t16_collision.json`.
The row that names the error is T-CO2, but it is not isolated: T-CO1's '32 boxes', T-P3's
'32 collision bodies … 11 driveable / 21 not' and the JSON's whole `bmak` block are the same
substitution at a different scale. T-CO3 and T-PA3 are clean because they quote JC3 donor
part-table values, not BmAK geometry.
