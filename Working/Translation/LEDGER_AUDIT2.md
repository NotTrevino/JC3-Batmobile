## Findings

1. **The `.physicsc` is fully readable.** 62 body-table entries (40 of them hulls; 54 hull objects in the file), 18 extern meshes, 2 dynamic and 2 static compounds; `bm_colhull.selftest` re-encodes 54/54 hulls byte for byte and `bm_colmesh.selftest` passes on this file. G3-11's UNKNOWN 3 was false.
1. **The world hull is provably the render mesh's hull.** Max LOD2 protrusion 2.1e-08 m over the 96-vertex body-0 polytope - the B-10c derivation is verified, not assumed.
1. **A root compound's AABB is short by 0.01593 m.** `0xd3c80` still carries the box B-10b refit; B-10c then replaced body 0's hull with a larger one and did not refit the tree. This is a measured inconsistency in the built file, and it is the same one the `hknpDynamicCompoundShapeData` refit shows.

1. **`.epe` rows are checkable by name.** 243 named objects; `name_hash` = `hash_jenkins(name)` holds on the ones sampled (the audit resolves 12 lights-module presets through it).
