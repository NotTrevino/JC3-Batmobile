## How a verdict is reached

* **RESOLVED** — every core card's report says `done`, no core card is under a `needs
  rework` review, and every evidence probe holds.
* **PARTIAL** — at least one evidence probe holds, or at least one core card is
  done/partial.
* **OPEN** — neither.

Labels: **decoded** = a value read from a named file at a named offset/export by the cited
card; **derived** = a rule over decoded values; **UNKNOWN** = not decoded, with what was
tried.
