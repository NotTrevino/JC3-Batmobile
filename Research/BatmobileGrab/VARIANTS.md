## 8. What the record shows (inputs for D-5, not the decision)

1. **A skin is not a palette.** V2 vs Joker: 61 materials and 54 textures differ (extra/missing counts above), 5 sockets differ, 40 bones separate the skins (220 vs 180).
2. **BmAK ships one vehicle per skin**: four packages, four `RVehicleBatmobile` objects,
   four `outer` archetypes (table at the top).
3. **Physics is shared** (§5): a drop entry per skin needs no new sim tuning.
4. **Joker is a different car in gameplay terms**: 5 gadgets against 10 (its own set adds `Batmobile_JokerCannon_V2`, `Batmobile_JokerHeavy_Cannon`, `Batmobile_JokerMissile_Launcher`, `Batmobile_JokerHeavy_Cannon_Projectile` and `Batmobile_JokerMissile_Projectile`, and it has no EMP, sonar, tracker, winch or drone hacker), a 17-row light rig with no `MaterialType` binding, no interior-lights flag, and a threat component.

The design consequence (tint vs extra drop entries) is a decision, not a data finding.
