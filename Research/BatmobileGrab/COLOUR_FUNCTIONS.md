## 3. What does survive: the parameter expressions

351 `MaterialExpression*Parameter` exports, 351 walk to `eof`, 2 without a name (663, 732), 153 without a value.

The parameter name's field offset is a **per-class** fact (`ScalarParameter` 0xac, `StaticSwitchParameter` 0xac, `TextureSampleParameter2D` 0x11c, `TextureSampleParameterFlipbook` 0x11c, `TextureSampleParameterNormal` 0x11c, `TextureSampleParameterSubUV` 0x11c, `VectorParameter` 0xac), which is why a fixed-offset search finds the scalars and misses every texture.

Values present in the streams: scalars **142/181** (FloatProperty +0xcc), vectors **0/87**. A `MaterialExpressionVectorParameter` serialises name, guid, bool and material but **no default colour at all** - 0/87. The vector colours exist only in the MIC chain (`COLOUR.json`), not in the master material graph as serialised here.
