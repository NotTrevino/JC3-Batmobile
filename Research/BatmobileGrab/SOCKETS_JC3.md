## Convention

- Positions are JC3 metres: X width, Y up, Z length, nose at **-Z**.
- A socket location is centimetres in its bone's local frame. The socket world point is `bone + qrot(bone q, local, inverse=True)`, then the one `to_jc3_rot(p) * 0.01 + offset` transform.
- `forward` is the socket's local +X (UE forward), and `up` is local +Y. Both go through socket rotation, bone bind rotation and the JC3 rotation.
- A null rotator is identity. A null location (`DriverAnimOrigin`) places the socket at its bone pivot.
- Every socket lists its bone and that bone's parent from `BONES.json`; `Batmobile_Chassis` is the root and its parent is itself.
