Generated by `Tools/scripts/grab_collision_boxes.py` (`--write`); `--check` re-derives it
from the packages and fails on a one-byte difference.

Decoded here: **3 boxes** in `BmGame.upk` (the drivable car's PhysicsAsset) and **128 boxes**
in the four wreck PhysicsAssets, used as the fleet oracle. Every byte of the two

Labels: **decoded** = read from bytes (package + export + byte offset); **derived** =
computed from decoded values with the rule given; **UNKNOWN** = not decoded, with what was

The wreck asset's boxes are per-bone envelopes of an exploded rig - the largest is 317.9422 cm on one axis while the translations span only [312.3, 79.3, 80.6] cm - so the assumption this comparison rests on (that those boxes tile the intact car) is not proven either way.
