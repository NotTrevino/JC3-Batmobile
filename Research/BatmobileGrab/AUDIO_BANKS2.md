  34,497 `AkBank` exports; this card searches the
  117 that `AUDIO_BANKS.md` lists), or
- a `BKPK` blob inside a `DLG_*` export payload (73 of the
  117 searched payloads: a bank at a non-zero offset plus a `BKPK` blob after it;
  `O-4` raw-searches that blob but it is not parsed as a container), or
- a `BKPK`-magic `.bnk` file (15 of them; the format is not parsed by this toolkit), or
- a bank whose Event id is renamed at cook time, in which case the declared id never exists.

It would be settled by scanning every `AkBank` export of the install for the id (the same
reader, 34,497 exports), or by a Wwise `SoundbanksInfo` manifest, which is not in this install.
