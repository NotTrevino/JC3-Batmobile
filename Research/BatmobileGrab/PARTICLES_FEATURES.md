## Method

- **Groups**: first name rule wins (see RULES in bmak_particle_features.py), `other` last; no system is left ungrouped.
- **Ranges**: min/max over every LOD level of every emitter of the system; a constant distribution contributes its value to both ends.
- **Spawn**: spawn_rate is ParticleModuleSpawn.Rate, required_spawn is ParticleModuleRequired.SpawnRate - both are the *continuous* rate distributions; a 0..0 range means emission comes from the module's burst list or from ParticleModuleSpawnPerUnit, which this pass does not extract.
- **Colours**: re-read through grab_particles.Walker: the two stored vectors of StartColor / ColorOverLife / ColorScaleOverLife, bounds taken per component (the cooked pair is not always min-first).
- **Materials**: ParticleModuleRequired's Material reference, resolved with Walker.path - PARTICLES.json's own `material` column is the required-module path, not the material.
