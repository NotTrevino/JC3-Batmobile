## 3. "Byte-identical": tested, not asserted

Each `AggGeom` payload is hashed (sha1 of the StructProperty payload as stored):

| package | bodies | distinct payload sha1 | identical to the others |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `Playable_Batmobile_SF` | 32 | 32 | no |
| `Playable_BatmobileMK2_SF` | 32 | 32 | no |
| `Playable_BatmobileJoker_SF` | 32 | 32 | no |
| `Playable_BatmobileFunhouse_SF` | 32 | 32 | no |

Payload multisets identical across all four: **False**.  Parsed triples identical: **True**.

**The old card's "byte-identical" sentence is false, and this is where it fails.**  The
payloads differ between the four packages at exactly **offset(s) [12, 13]** - a single u32 at
+12, whose value the old docstring dismissed as "constant 2846 on every record": {"Playable_Batmobile_SF": [2846], "Playable_BatmobileMK2_SF": [2851], "Playable_BatmobileJoker_SF": [3120], "Playable_BatmobileFunhouse_SF": [2836]}.
Every other byte matches, which is why the parsed extents and centres agree on all 32
bones and why a value-level comparison could not see the difference.
