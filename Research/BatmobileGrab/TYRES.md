# Batmobile tyres — the three diameters (G1-18)

Generated by `Tools/scripts/bmak_tyres.py` from `Batmobile_V2.pskx`, **vertices owned by
the wheel bones**, in metres through the ONE transform (`bat_assemble.to_jc3_rot`, then ×
`scale` 0.01 + `offset` from `Working/TrueScale/stations.json`) — the same transform
`bat_assemble.py` uses for the whole car, so these numbers are directly comparable to
`T_ROWS.jsonl` T-G1/T-G2 and to JC3's `land_global.*_wheels.radius`. `--check` re-derives
every number below and compares this page with the stored file.

Axes in that frame (MODLOG §70): **x = axle (lateral, left = −x), y = vertical, z =
longitudinal**; ground is y = 0.02.

All four tyres are the same mould to **0.5 mm** (tread Ø 1.3652–1.3657, shoulder Ø 1.3711–1.3714,
width 0.7314–0.7325 m). Each tread Ø is 2 × the corner's `stations.json` `radius` to ≤ 0.6 mm
(2 × 0.6826–0.6829 = 1.3652–1.3658); the tread sits on the ground plane (rear +0.0000, front +0.0021).

**Station `tyre_width` is mirrored L↔R in `stations.json`**: the file's f_l 0.7325 is the mesh's
f_r width 0.7325, and the file's f_r 0.7314 is the mesh's f_l 0.7314 (rears likewise). Nothing in
`stations.json` records a `tyre_bone`, so the derivation cannot be traced; whoever consumes
`tyre_width` for physics must check the corner attribution (R3).

**Rim** (front-left): `Wheel_Frnt_Left_Fan`, 32 faces. The figure quoted as rim Ø is the **max radial
distance from the hub**, 0.5127 → **Ø 1.0254**; the cap is dished, so its own y/z extent is
1.0096 / 1.0095 m, not the same measure.

**Tyre bead aperture and sidewall ring: UNKNOWN.** The earlier pass quoted Ø1.135–1.360 and
1.322–1.371 for them, but gave no vertex-selection rule, so neither can be re-derived from
this page's inputs. No number is printed rather than an unreproducible one (R4).

## 2. The three numbers

| number | what it actually is | source |
|---|---|---|
| **1.366** | **tread-to-tread through the hub, and 2 × the physics radius** — the same number by design | this page: tread Ø 1.3652–1.3657 = 2 × `stations.json` `radius` 0.6826–0.6829 (≤ 0.6 mm, above) = T-G2's `land_global.*_wheels.radius` 0.68275 / 0.68265, which T-G6 keeps as the JC3 contact radius (its cell: keep the mesh radius: 0.64 m is BmAK's contact-radius simplification inside a 0.683 m visual tyre, and the JC3 radius sets both contact and ride height) |
| **1.40** | the **lore reference** — neither mesh nor physics | 4 ft 7 in = 1.397 m wiki estimate, user-verified against footage with Batman at 188 cm (MODLOG §70) |
| **1.42** | **no source in the repo** — the initial-commit `bat_assemble.py` docstring figure | written in `7a0a07d`; `git log -S "1.42 m"` returns no other commit and no file measures 1.42. What the docstring measured is **UNKNOWN** (§3 prints the quantities near it) |

T-G2 does **not** carry a rear-tyre figure: its cells are "tyre Ø 1.366 m (mesh)", "`land_global.front/rear_wheels.radius`", decoded, "0.68275 / 0.68265".
No repo file records a rear diameter different from the front, and all four tyres measure
1.3652–1.3657 — a separate rear figure was in the earlier version of this page and is gone.

### The sidewall question D-1 asked

D-1: keep the PSKX true scale "unless G1-18 shows the 1.366 excludes the sidewall bulge".
It does exclude the bulge, and the bulge is small:

| corner | tread Ø (y) | Ø max radial (shoulder) | bulge |
|---|---|---|---|
| f_l | 1.3652 | 1.3711 | 5.9 mm |
| f_r | 1.3657 | 1.3714 | 5.7 mm |
| r_l | 1.3654 | 1.3712 | 5.8 mm |
| r_r | 1.3652 | 1.3711 | 5.9 mm |

Per corner the shoulder adds **5.7–5.9 mm** on its own tread; against the four-corner mean
tread Ø 1.3654 the largest shoulder is 6.0 mm higher. Reaching 1.40 from the shoulder (Ø 1.3714)
needs ×1.021 (+2.1%); from the tread (Ø 1.3654) it needs ×1.025 (+2.5%) — 0.0346 m, i.e. 34.6 mm of rubber that is not in the mesh.
Ø including the shoulder = **1.3714**, still 2.1% short of 1.40 on that basis. None of the
three headline numbers is "the bulge number" — that number is 1.3714.

## 5. Decision D-1 — resolved: keep the PSKX true scale

**No rescale of the car.** The measurement closes D-1's condition:

1. the tyre reads Ø1.3652–1.3657 tread-to-tread and Ø1.3711–1.3714 including the shoulder — the bulge is 5.7–5.9 mm, far short of the 34.6 mm needed to reach 1.40;
2. 1.42 has no source in the repo, and what the initial docstring measured is UNKNOWN;
3. T-G6 already keeps the mesh radius as the JC3 physics radius, so ride height, contact and
   track are unchanged; the whole car would move together through the one transform (R2) if
   it ever were rescaled.

DIM-2 ("tyre diameter matches the lore reference") is satisfied as **1.365 measured vs 1.40
reference, 2.5% apart, accepted**: the mesh is the physics source and the wiki number is
an estimate. Nothing in the repo is edited to make the tyre read 1.40.

## 6. How to re-measure

```
python Tools/scripts/bmak_tyres.py --check
```

It re-reads the PSKX, `stations.json`, T-G2/T-G6 and `CHAIN.json`, re-derives every table
above and fails if this page differs from the render or an invariant breaks (four corners,
tread spread ≤ 0.6 mm, each tread Ø = 2 × station radius within 1.5 mm, tread on the ground
within 0.4 cm).
