## 4. Why the verdicts are mostly `still open`

A row is `holds` only when a producer line states the row's own quantity and carries its
number; a coincident count is not enough (T-PA7's 20 deform points are not the 20 parts
the row says the deform solver skips).  That test is met by:

- `T-P2`: one printed line states the row's quantity and carries its number(s) (42, 17)

- `T-PA2`: one printed line states the row's quantity and carries its number(s) (21, 12)

The other 33 rows assert a translation value, a class choice or a per-member quantity that
the cited producer validates but never prints: the seven scripts print their own check
counts, not the row's number.  That is a finding about `LEDGER_SOURCES.md`, not a failure
of the rows: "re-derivable" there means *the artefact the row rests on has a green
producer*, and this pass adds that a green producer does not by itself re-print the value.
No row is marked `wrong value`, because no fresh run printed a contradicting number for
the same quantity.
