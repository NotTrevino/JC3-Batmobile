## The reference skeleton, decoded

The cooked SkeletalMesh stores an `FReferenceSkeleton` as 44-byte records. The fields sit **before** the bone name, which is why a name-anchored read of `+32` lands on the next bone's position:

| record offset | bytes | content | evidence |
|---:|---:|---|---|
| -28 | 16 | quat (x, y, z, w), parent-relative | orthonormal on every record |
| -12 | 12 | position, parent-relative (cm) | reproduces the PSK bind pose |
| 0 | 8 | FName (name index, number=0) | 194 names resolve in both meshes |
| +8 | 4 | parent index | matches the PSK parent on 180/180 |
| +12 | 4 | `-1` on every record | constant in both meshes |

Layout oracle: the same read on the intact `Batmobile_V2` mesh reproduces BONES.json's PSK bind pose - **180/180** local positions (worst 0.0000 cm), **180/180** parent indices, **180/180** component positions after accumulation (worst 0.0251 cm), **180/180** local rotations. The PSK export is the Y-mirrored handedness, so Y is negated in the comparison.

Wreck skeleton: `Batmobile_V3_Destroyed` export, all four packages, 32 bones, and **every bone is a direct child of `Root`** (flat), so a bone's component position is its stored position.
