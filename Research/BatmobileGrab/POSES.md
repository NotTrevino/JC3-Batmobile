## Convention

- Positions are JC3 metres: X width, Y up, Z length with the nose at **-Z**.
- Bone poses are reconstructed from the PSK left-handed convention (`world = q_child * q_parent`, child offset rotated by the inverse parent).
- The one transform is `to_jc3_rot(p) * 0.01 + offset`; rotations keep their angle and rotate their axis through the same proper rotation.
- A missing animation channel falls back to that bone's bind local transform; the key-frame export marks removed channels null, not zero.
