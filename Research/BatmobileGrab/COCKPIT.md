(`bat_assemble.to_jc3_rot` x `stations.json` `scale` + `offset`), the `Driver` socket, `SLOTS.json`, `stations.json` and the material rows. Every number below is produced by that
script; `--check` re-derives it and compares (`Research/BatmobileGrab/COCKPIT.json`). Nothing
here is hand-typed.

Frame: **x = axle (left = -x), y = vertical (ground 0.02), z = longitudinal, nose at -z**.

Value classes (R4) are given per table: `read` (socket/slot/material inputs), `measured`
(triangles or rays on the PSKX), `derived` (arithmetic on those), `assumed` (anthropometry,
no source - the eye point is `derived (assumed input)`).

## 5. APP-3 - what is visible through the glass

The interior is enclosed by the windscreen ahead (z -1.9143), the side windows beside the driver's head (x -0.2942 / 0.2943, z -1.5925..-0.8349) and an **opaque roof** (liner `Batmobile_Worn_NoRain` + skin) directly overhead. Seat and screens are therefore visible **through the windscreen and the side windows, not from above**.

The glass is tinted and alpha-mapped (`Glass_Opacity=1.2000, Glass_Reflectivity=0.8000, Glass_Roughness=1.1000, glass_smudges=0.2000`), and the interior glow comes from the seat material's emissive map (`Textures.Batmobile_V3_Int_Cockpit_E` x `15.0000`).

## 6. Gaps - stated, not smoothed

1. **No yoke / steering control.** The hand box |x| < 0.17, y 1.00..1.30, z -1.60..-1.10 contains **4 faces of any material** (y 0.90..1.00: 24, y 1.00..1.10: 0, y 1.10..1.20: 0, y 1.20..1.30: 4). The nearest driver-facing surface is the instrument panel at z -1.5097. Consequence for ANI-9: the seated pose has nothing to grip; a grip point, if one is wanted, must come from the JC3 side (G2-19).
2. **No eye point in any file** (section 4) - the eye above is derived from an assumed seated eye height, not read.
3. **Screen content is not in the material.** `Cockpit_Master_Flip_Data` is a master `Material` with no connected Diffuse/Specular/Power/Emissive and no texture parameters - the 0.3188 x 0.1512 m panel geometry ships, the picture does not. What glows comes from the seat material's emissive map, not from a screen texture.
4. **No other interior mesh.** `MESHES.md` lists 12 mesh exports; apart from `Batmobile_V2` they are the Destroyed/Fire variants, the MK2 and Jokerfied skins and the Batwing carrier, so the yoke is not hiding elsewhere.

## 7. How to re-measure

```
python Tools/scripts/grab_cockpit.py --write    # COCKPIT.json + COCKPIT.md
python Tools/scripts/grab_cockpit.py --check    # re-derive and compare
```

The script ray-casts the seat, roof, glass and sightlines (Moeller-Trumbore, both windings),
counts faces per slot and per bone, prints the panel/hand-box/side-window selections with
their rules, and reads the socket and material rows. `--check` fails if any of them moves.
