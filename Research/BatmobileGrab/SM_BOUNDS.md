# StaticMesh Bounds of the Batmobile's two collision meshes (G3-01b U-4)

Generated by `Tools/scripts/grab_sm_bounds.py --write`. Every number below is recomputed from `BmGame.upk` by `--check`, which also fails if this file or the JSON differs by one byte.

**The question.** G3-01b (`grab_collision_boxes.py`) wanted the cooked `StaticMesh` bounds of `Batmobile_V2` (175973) and `Batmobile_V2_Collision_Pursuite` (175974) as a second check on its box extents. It found no `Bounds` struct in their tagged properties - correctly: both exports carry **four** tags and end the property list at byte 32.

**The answer.** The bounds are in the untagged native data that starts at byte **34** (both exports), as an `FBoxSphereBounds`: Origin (3 floats), BoxExtent (3 floats), SphereRadius (1 float) = 28 bytes.

**Verdict: FULL lengths**, by the two chassis boxes (the whole-car envelopes). Under the full-length reading every chassis-box dimension is at most **1.0643** of the mesh span; under the half-length reading each chassis box's long axis would be **1.9073** of the span of the mesh that is supposed to contain it. This reproduces G3-01b's own result ("extents: FULL lengths") from the mesh geometry instead of from the convex hull.

The test is run on each box's longest axis, not on its smallest: chassis box 1 is a floor pan, 103.5 cm on one axis, so under the half reading that axis alone would still be 0.7067 of the mesh span and decide nothing. The long axis is where the two readings are a factor of two apart.

The weapons box (169751, `Batmobile_Rig_Weapons_Trans`) is a part, not the car, so its ratios (~0.14-0.39) decide nothing and are listed without a verdict.

## 6. What this changes for G3-01b

- G3-01b's U-4 ("the StaticMesh Bounds as a second extents oracle") is **answered**: the bounds are a decoded `FBoxSphereBounds` in the native data, not a tagged property, and they agree with the full-length reading of the box extents.
- The mesh bounds give the two collision meshes' own extents: battle **712.6005 / 491.2594 / 292.9303 cm**, pursuit **705.6577 / 351.8964 / 233.3241 cm** (full spans), with sphere radii **434.8802 / 382.2412 cm**.
- Still open, and not guessed here: the *member names* of the two neighbouring fields (U-1), the exact vertex set behind SphereRadius (U-2), and why 13 other StaticMesh exports in the package do not carry the block at the same offset (U-3).
