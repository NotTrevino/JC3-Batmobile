### Why `-` removes by exact struct, not by `Name`

`-` lines are rare (7 in the whole chain) and only one of them removes anything at all:

| line | struct | exact matches before it | same-Name different struct |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `DefaultInput.ini:71` | `Name="XboxTypeS_A",Command="SetFreezeRendering"` | 1 (BaseInput.ini:69) | 0 |

That line deletes the Base `XboxTypeS_A` entry and keeps every other `XboxTypeS_A` binding,
which is exactly the name-based reading failing: with `-` by `Name` the merge matches only
208 of 366 entries, and the first divergence is the loss of the Base/F5 entry. The other six
`-` lines name entries that do not exist in the chain and are no-ops either way.

### Why `.` is recorded as *append*

`.` cannot be told apart from "append, or replace an identical entry" on this data:
**242** `.` lines exist in the chain and **0** of them has a canonical body that already
exists, so both readings produce the same array. `clear` is refuted outright (it drops the
Base entries the oracle keeps) and `replace by Name` is refuted (it loses 158 entries). The
rule recorded in
`BINDINGS_MERGED.json` is therefore: **plain = append; `+` = not observed (UNKNOWN);
`-` = remove the entry whose canonical struct matches; `.` = append** (append and
replace-exact are the same function on this data).
