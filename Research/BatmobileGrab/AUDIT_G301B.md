### 4.1 The one blocking point

The mode review's I1 said the selector *is* referenced by UnrealScript and the card's
"native, unreferenced" conclusion was false. Two later cards settle it: `COLLISION_SELECTOR.md`
decodes export 10894 and its value -> mesh table, and `SELECTOR_POLARITY.md` decides the
branch polarity (body runs when the comparison is true; 4 of 4 decisive cases, 6 of 6
cases in total). `COLLISION_MODES.md` itself was never revised, so its three UNKNOWN
verdicts still print - the answer exists, the card does not carry it.

### 4.2 Where the reviews and the later cards disagree

- **boxes review I-3** proposed that the 4.00 deg tilt's sign is fixed because the planes are
  rows. `MATRIX_ORDER.md` (later than the review's target cards) reports **row/column order
  UNSETTLED**: the build stores no transform both as a 64-byte FMatrix and as a quaternion,
  and the one candidate (the mesh reference pose) is the identity. So `COLLISION_BOXES.md`'s
  conditional +/-4.00 deg is the correct state, and the review's proposed fix is not adopted.
- **boxes review I-1/I-2** are fixed, but by different later cards than the ones the review
  named: I-1 by `BOX_FLAGS.md` (a 4648-box census, bit 6 = `bInterVehicleCollision`) and I-2
  by `COLLISION_SHAPES.md` (25 bodies with spheres, 271 with capsules, 3276 bodies closing).

  not established. It would be settled by an `RB_ConstraintSetup` layout (G3-06) or by a
  second constraint setup with a known field set.
- **The Bounds arrays** (PhysicsAsset 72 B / 2 elements; StaticMesh 175973/175974). Both
  stay UNKNOWN in `COLLISION_BOXES.md` and `COLLISION_CONVEX.md`; the SDK tree ships no
  StaticMesh layout either.
- **The 0x4C bit's behaviour.** The name (`bInterVehicleCollision`) comes from the SDK layout
  and the census, not from an observed effect; `Batmobile_Chassis` box 0 is the only drivable
  box with bit 6 set, so the flag is present but its consequence is unobserved.
- **The 4.00 deg tilt's sign** (rows vs columns). `MATRIX_ORDER.md`: UNSETTLED.
- **Which collision component is attached at runtime.** The setter's branch is decoded, the
  sampler logs the mode value, but no card logs an attachment, so the mode -> mesh step is
  still an inference from the value -> mesh table.
