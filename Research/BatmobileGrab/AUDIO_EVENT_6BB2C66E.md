## Answer

**The event has a name and a declared bank, and the shipped bank does not cook it.**

- id: `0x6BB2C66E`.
- `AUDIO_MAP.json` names it **`%s`** (the Wwise event name) and declares it to
  `Engine.CHR_BatmanDisruptor`.
- the cooked package's **name table carries a different string**, **`%s`**, and
  that is the `AkEvent` object that fires.
- the 117 bank payloads that carry every referenced BmAK bank were searched four ways
  (parsed HIRC ids, raw u32 little-endian, raw u32 big-endian, ASCII name):
  **0 of 117** payloads carry `0x6BB2C66E`.
- the alternate name hashes to **`0x8EA6FCA0`**, and that id is carried by **`BmGame.CHR_BatmanDisruptor`, `StartupPatch.CHR_BatmanDisruptor`**.

So the answer to *"which bank holds 0x6BB2C66E"* is: none, and the reason is narrower than
"the bank is missing". The declared bank ships (`CHR_BatmanDisruptor`, 7 rows / 2 ids in
`AUDIO_MAP.json`, present in `BmGame` and `StartupPatch`); it cooks the tracker-deploy
event and it **does not cook the vehicle-tracking-fire event**. Nothing is silent by
accident of search - the event id is absent from the bytes, and the object that carries
the name is the declaration side, not the play side.
