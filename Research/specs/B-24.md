# B-24 build spec — the held lock (2.0 s) and the 8-target salvo

Card source: `tmp/freebuff/reports/wp4-fire-leech.md` §3.4/§4/§6 (T-WP4), `T_WP3B_LOCKON.md`
§4.1/§8.5, ledger rows `T-WP4` (`next`, launcher) and `T-WP15` (`out`, disable). Opened
2026-11-06 by `tmp/freebuff/reports/apply-weapon-slot-rows.md`.

**SUMMARY: NO MEMBER ON EITHER SIDE.** BmAK's missile behaviour holds a lock for
`MissileLockOnDuration` **2.0 s** (civilian 1.0 s) and fires at `MaxTargets` **8**. JC3's weapon
data has no duration and no target count: every missile tune carries `missile_per_shot` **1**, the
whole `AutoAimSettings` block is 22 members with no timer, and `missile_loose_lock_distance` is a
*range*, not a time. Both are native behaviours, so the card's job is to author the closest system
and say plainly what it cannot reproduce.

## 1. The numbers that have no carrier

| BmAK member | where decoded | value | JC3 member |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `RBatmobileEMPMissile.MissileLockOnDuration` | launcher `0x0A00` | **2.0 s** (civilian 1.0) | **none** — `AutoAim` = 22 members, 0 durations |
| `MaxTargets` | launcher | **8** | **none** — `missile_per_shot` 1 in all 7 missile tunes |
| `MissileNumberParameter` | launcher | `ref:Ww_Parameters.BML_NumberOfTargets` (value in no file) | **none** |
| `TargetSwitchName` (LoseLockXBonus 600 / YBonus 800) | `RBatmobileVehicleTracker` | switch-while-locked | **none** |
| `LoseLockTime` | tracker | not serialised in the 3 packages | **none** |

## 2. What JC3 does ship, and why it is not the same thing

- **Salvo:** `burst_count` / `burst_cooldown` exist (w614 fires 6 × 5 s) but at **one target** —
  `missile_per_shot` 1 and nothing names a target list. A "salvo of 8 at 8 targets" is 8 weapons.
- **Hold:** the lock is acquisition-only. `StickyAimStrength` is **0.0** in every tune (including
  the Fire Leech), so there is no magnet and therefore no hold to time.
- **Timer:** no `AutoAim` or `MissileWeaponTuningBase` member is a duration except
  `missile_detonation_time` (-1 = never) and `missile_no_turning_time` (0), both projectile-side.

## 3. Steps (closest system, in the build)

1. Author the BmAK numbers **where they can live**: `AutoAim.MaxAimDistance` 120 m (the lock range)
   and the acquisition cone 0.12 / 0.15 on the new lock-on tune (T-WP4). That is the whole of the
   lock that data can carry.
2. Reproduce the **salvo** as 8 slot-able missile weapons sharing one aim point (T-WP13's forced
   slots already provide the 8 roots): each is a `fire_rate`-gated shot, not a multi-target split.
   Record the difference on the row — 8 weapons at one aim point ≠ 8 targets selected by a lock.
3. The **2.0 s hold** stays a named omission. If a script route is ever found, it is a timed gate on
   the fire action, not a weapon member.
