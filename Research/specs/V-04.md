# V-04 build spec — Audio gates

Card (`GRAB_BACKLOG.md` line 1125):

```
#### V-04 · Audio gates   S · deps: BX
Do: decode the built bank back; compare each sample to its WAV source (length ± 1 frame);
loudness within ± 1 LU of the vanilla event it replaces; loops seamless (no click > −40 dBFS at
the loop point).
Done: all events pass.
```

**SUMMARY: BLOCKED.** The decode and the loudness gate are real and one of them already runs; the
**loop gate cannot be evaluated at all**, and the length gate as written is contradicted by B-11's own
design.

- **Decode-back exists.** `python Tools/scripts/bm_audio.py --check` already re-parses the built bank
  (`fsb_of`), walks each written sample (`vgm`), re-hashes its payload (`payload_sha256`), checks
  frames, name, duration and the event/tune GUIDs in `TLNS/TMLN/TLNB` — and prints
  `PASS: 5 mapped rows, 5 slots in 2 banks (1877 samples), 5 events resolved, max LU delta 0.00 <= 0.05`
  (exit 0). It just reads `Builds/bm-004`, not an RC.
- **Loudness ±1 LU is measurable and currently green** — the map itself calls `delta_lu` "the ±1 LU
  gate" (`t12_audio_map.json.method.gain`); the five chosen slots read `0.0, 0.0, 0.0, −0.2, −0.57`.
- **The length criterion cannot hold against the raw WAV.** B-11 trims, pads and time-stretches every
  payload on purpose. Measured against the WAV sources: `vehicles#7` 29,954 vs 33,279 (−3,325),
  `#98` 24,933 vs 27,894 (−2,961), `#151` 154,818 vs 183,289 (−28,471), `#279` 336,000 vs 186,497
  (**+149,503**, `padded_frames`), `weapons#408` 160,337 vs 80,124 (**+80,213**, `stretch_ratio`
  2.0011). "± 1 frame of its WAV source" would fail all five by design; the honest referent is the
  derived span (`source_frames`, `trimmed_frames`, `padded_frames`, `stretch_ratio`), and the card's
  wording has to be restated, not satisfied.
- **The loop gate has no loop point.** All **34** `t12_audio_map.json` slots and all **5**
  `bm_bank_manifest.json` slots carry `loop_start: null, loop_end: null`, and the map states why:
  "no measured slot carries an FSB5 loop field (`loop_start` is null on every one); whether the loop
  is then produced by the event/tune graph, or this bank version simply carries no loop metadata, was
  not measured — **UNKNOWN**" (`t12_audio_map.json.method.loop`). This matters most where the card
  points: **3 of the 5 events are loops** — `jc3_veh_land_premium_nitro_loop`,
  `jc3_veh_lave_chassis_spin_creaks_loop_fa`, `jc3_wpn_minigun_fire_close_loop_04` — and two of them
  were time-stretched 1.80× and 2.00× and zero-padded by 149,503 and 80,213 frames, i.e. exactly the
  operations that create a seam. There is also **no click metric anywhere in `Tools/scripts`**
  (`click`, `seamless`, `discontinuity` → 0 hits in `bm_audio.py`, `bmak_audio_features.py`,
  `jc3audio.py`).
- **"the vanilla event it replaces" is name-matched, not proven.** The five chosen slots carry
  `slot_confidence: null` (`t12_audio_map.json.slots[].chosen`), while the map's own
  `method.slot` says a slot is either `proven` (test-060) or a `candidate` name match. The ±1 LU
  comparison is therefore against the *slot's* loudness — which is the shipped bank's payload, not an
  independently identified vanilla event.

## 3. Blockers

- **V-04/1 — no RC.** `Builds/bm-RC1/` missing; the card's "built bank" has a stand-in
  (`bm-004/banks`) but no release candidate and no staged `bm-RC1/banks/` (B-14 `todo`,
  `GRAB_BACKLOG.md:1341`).
- **V-04/2 — loop gate unmeasurable (the card's most load-bearing criterion).** No slot has a loop
  point (34/34 and 5/5 null); `t12_audio_map.json.method.loop` records the FSB5 loop field as absent
  and the mechanism as UNKNOWN; no click detector exists. 3 of 5 events are `_loop` samples, two of
  them stretched 1.80×/2.00× and padded by 149,503/80,213 frames. A seam test at an *invented* loop
  point would be a guess (R4) — this must be reported as `UNKNOWN`, not as a pass.
- **V-04/3 — the length criterion contradicts the build.** Measured deltas against the raw WAVs:
  `−3,325 / −2,961 / −28,471 / +149,503 / +80,213` frames (`bm_bank_manifest.json.slots[].frames`
  vs the source WAV). B-11's design (trim + stretch + pad, `t12_audio_map.json.method.stretch`)
  cannot satisfy "± 1 frame of its WAV source". The gate must compare against `source_frames`
  adjusted by `trimmed_frames`/`padded_frames`/`stretch_ratio`, and the card must be read that way.
- **V-04/4 — "the vanilla event it replaces" is unproven.** `slot_confidence` is `null` for all five
  chosen slots; the ±1 LU gate compares `delivered_lufs` to the *slot's* `lufs` (the shipped payload),
  which is only the vanilla event if the slot choice is `proven`. Today it is a name match
  (`method.slot`).
- **V-04/5 — the gate reads the wrong tree.** `bm_audio.py --check` is pinned to `TAG = "bm-004"`
  (line 74); nothing takes a bank directory. The RC gate needs `--banks DIR` (or `--build`).
- **V-04/6 — sources live outside the repo.** 4 of 5 WAVs are under `E:\Modding\_BmAKgrab\audio\wav\`
  (R10 — correct, but the gate depends on that tree and must fail loudly if a source is missing
  rather than skip).

## 4. Plan

### 4.1 Files to create

| file | role |
|---|---|
| `Tools/scripts/v04_audio.py` | the gate: `--decode`, `--length`, `--loudness`, `--loop`, `--check` (all four); reads `Builds/bm-RC1/banks/` (falls back to `bm-004/banks/` only with `--allow-bm004`), writes `Builds/bm-RC1/verify/audio.json` + `audio.md` |
| `Working/Audio/v04_loop_points.json` | the loop-point evidence file: per `_loop` slot either a measured `{start_frame, end_frame, source}` or `{"status": "UNKNOWN", "why": "no FSB5 loop field; t12 method.loop"}`. Written by the gate, never assumed |
| `Builds/bm-RC1/verify/audio.json` | the card's Done artefact: one row per event with `frames_src`, `frames_built`, `lufs_built`, `lufs_slot`, `delta_lu`, `loop_status`, `seam_dbfs` |

Reuses, no fork: `bm_audio.fsb_of`/`vgm`, `bmak_audio_features.integrated_lufs`,
`jc3audio.fev/fev_events/fev_tunes`.

### 4.2 Steps

0. **Preconditions.** `--check` requires a bank pair (RC first), the manifest and the map; every
   `source` WAV must exist, else `FAIL: source missing <path>` (exit 1). Missing RC →
   `BLOCKED: Builds/bm-RC1/banks missing (B-14/B-11)` exit 2.
1. **G1 decode.** For each written slot: open the bank, `fsb_of`, assert sample count 1877 total,
   locate `index`, re-hash the payload (`payload_sha256`), compare `frames`, `name`, `channels`,
   `rate`, and the event/tune GUID in the FEV tables. (This is `bm_audio.py --check`'s logic, aimed at
   the RC.) Prints `decode 5/5 slots, 1877 samples, payload sha256 5/5, events 5/5`.
2. **G2 length.** `frames_built` vs the derived source span:
   `round(source_frames × stretch_ratio) + padded_frames − trimmed_frames`; pass if
   `|difference| ≤ 1` frame. Prints `length 5/5 within 1 frame (max delta 1)` — the map's own numbers
   give `#151` trim 1, `#7` stretch 0.9001, `#279` stretch 1.8016 + 149,503 pad.
3. **G3 loudness.** `integrated_lufs` on the decoded PCM (mono downmix, as the swap writes it) vs the
   slot's `lufs` (vanilla payload) and vs `delivered_lufs`; pass if both `|delta| ≤ 1.0 LU`, print
   each. Today's map values: `0.0, 0.0, 0.0, −0.2, −0.57` — all inside ±1.
4. **G4 loop.** For each slot whose name contains `_loop` (3 of 5):
   - If `v04_loop_points.json` holds measured points → compute the seam statistic at that point:
     `20·log10(|x[end] − x[start]| / full_scale)` and the max sample-step within ±2 ms of the seam
     vs the 99th percentile of interior steps; fail if the seam step exceeds −40 dBFS **and** the
     interior baseline is lower.
   - If no points exist (today) → `loop_status: "UNKNOWN"` with the `method.loop` citation, and the
     gate **exits 1** (not 0) — the card's "all events pass" cannot be claimed.
   Always print the file-level wrap statistic (`x[last]` vs `x[0]`) as a diagnostic, labelled
   "file wrap (not the engine's loop point)".
5. **G5 report.** `audio.json`/`audio.md`; exit 0 only if G1–G3 pass and every loop slot is either
   measured-passing or absent (no `_loop` slot exists).

### 4.3 Ordering

G1 first (needs decodable slots), then G2/G3 on the decoded PCM, G4 independent of G3. No step writes
to a bank.

## 6. Tamper tests the `--check` must catch

1. Re-encode one slot 2 frames shorter → G2 `length` fails naming the slot.
2. Apply +2 LU gain to one payload → G3 fails (`delta_lu 2.x`).
3. Overwrite 16 payload bytes with zeros → G1 payload-sha256 fails.
4. Insert a 0.3 s silence at the middle of a `_loop` slot's data and set a measured loop point around
   it → G4 seam fails.
5. Delete one source WAV → `source missing`, exit 1 (not a skip).
6. Point `--banks` at `bm-004` without `--allow-bm004` → refuses, exit 2.
7. Remove one GUID from the manifest and keep it in the bank → G1 `events 4/5`.

## 7. What stays UNKNOWN

- Every loop point, and whether these three `_loop` events are looped by the bank or by the
  event/tune graph (`t12_audio_map.json.method.loop`).
- Whether the slot chosen for each of the 5 events is `proven` or a `candidate` name match
  (`slot_confidence` null) — i.e. what "the vanilla event it replaces" exactly is.
- Whether the 1.8×/2.0× stretched, padded loops are audible as artefacts (a measurement can flag the
  seam; only a listen or phase Q can judge it).
- Whether the merged RC's `fmod_guids` list matches the shipped `vehicle_common` expectations.
