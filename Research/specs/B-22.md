# B-22 build spec — droop and up-travel: the two travels JC3 has no member for

Card source: `tmp/freebuff/reports/suspension-unit-bridge.md` §7/§9 (T-S6), ledger row `T-S6`
(`next`), `Working/Translation/T3_COVERAGE.md`. Companion card: `B-23.md` (per-mode and
stationary rate). Opened 2026-11-06 by `tmp/freebuff/reports/apply-suspension-rows.md`.

**SUMMARY: ONE MEMBER IS PLACEABLE, ONE IS NOT.** JC3's `Suspension` module carries a single
`length` per axle. BmAK splits travel into `SuspensionTravelUp` **30** cm and
`SuspensionTravelDown` **40** cm and adds `BMRearWheelMaxDroop` **100** cm. The **down** half is
placed by T-S6's bridge (`length` 0.40 m, fit error +5.8% / −5.5%). The **up** half has no second
travel: the closest member is `max_length_offset` (m), which is **0 on all 166** fleet vehicles and
whose engine semantics are UNKNOWN. The **droop** has no channel at all: 1.0 m of droop is 2.5x the
compression travel, and no decoded member names a droop or a rebound stop.

## 1. The BmAK numbers (decoded)

| member | value | source |
|---|---:|---|
| `SuspensionTravelDown` (all four wheels) | **40** cm = 0.40 m | `Research/BatmobileGrab/TYRES_PACKAGE.md` |
| `SuspensionTravelUp` (fronts; rears serialise down only) | **30** cm = 0.30 m | same |
| `BMRearWheelMaxDroop` | **100** cm = 1.0 m | same |
| `WheelRadius` | 64 cm (the cm unit proof: 0.64 m) | same |
| `BattleModeLength` / `PursuitModeWidth` | 430.0 / 220.0 cm (4.30 / 2.20 m) | `CLASS_DEFAULTS.md` |

## 2. What JC3 can hold

| member | fleet presence | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `Suspension.front.length` / `rear.length` | 166/166, 0.120–1.500 m | the only travel; T-S6 fills it with 0.40 m |
| `Suspension.*.min_length` | **0 on all 166** | never authored; semantics UNKNOWN |
| `Suspension.*.max_length_offset` | **0 on all 166** | closest carrier for the up-travel; m; never authored |
| (droop / rebound stop) | **no member** | 0 rows in the 166-file vocabulary carry droop, rebound, bump-stop or droop-limit |

Oracle: bridge report `--check` oracles `no_droop_member` (PASS, "min_length and max_length_offset 0
on all") and `travel_down_inside_fleet_band` (PASS, length/radius 0.625 vs p5 0.319 / p95 2.031).

## 3. Steps

1. **Length decision first.** T-S3 still declares 0.70 m (`SuspensionTravelUp` + `TravelDown`); the
   bridge fits 0.40 m better (length/radius p51.6 against p82.5) and at 0.70 m the implied Batmobile
   mass is 388.9 kg (**+85.2%** against the decoded 210 kg). Do not write `max_length_offset` before
   this is settled, or the two travels will be counted twice.
2. **Up-travel:** author `max_length_offset` **0.30** on both axles in a private `suspension.vmodc`
   copy, keeping `length` 0.40 m and `min_length` 0. Probe whether the wheel can extend past its rest
   position at all; if the engine ignores the member (UNKNOWN today), record the reader as absent and
   keep the travel split as a declared gap.
3. **Droop:** nothing to write. Keep 1.0 m recorded as a BmAK value with no JC3 carrier, and treat
   the rear axle's extension limit as an omission of the build, not a translation.
4. **Rebuild read-back:** re-decode the two axles (`Tools/scripts/jc3adf.py`) and confirm
   `length` 0.40 / `max_length_offset` 0.30 / `min_length` 0.

## 4. Done (gate BZ-1)

- The built `suspension` module reads back `max_length_offset` 0.30 on both axles and `length` 0.40.
- A take measures rest ride height and a full-compression landing; the wheel neither sinks past the
  0.40 m stop nor floats above it.
- The droop omission is written on the row (`T-S6`) with the 1.0 m BmAK number beside it.

## 5. Stop

If the engine ignores `max_length_offset` (no wheel extension observed), record the member as
write-only/UNKNOWN, leave `length` at 0.40 m, and keep the up-travel + droop as named omissions —
neither is a translation defect.
