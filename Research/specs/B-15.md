# B-15 build spec — the riot (non-lethal) mode's carrier and its gap

Card source: `tmp/freebuff/reports/non-lethal-cannon-carrier.md` (WPN-10), operator rule
2026-11-06 ("compromises are not accepted when a lore-accurate route exists"), ledger rows
`T-WP10` and `T-WP14` in `Working/Translation/T_ROWS.jsonl`.

**SUMMARY: BUILDABLE (closest-system case).** The report found **no** non-lethal carrier: no
stun/taser/shock/tranquilliser/bean-bag/ragdoll/water-cannon member exists in any decoded tune or
module, and the `electricity` channel is vehicle armour with no weapon→channel map. The nearest
mechanism is a **private bullet tune with `damage = 0` and the BmAK impulse**; the `riot ↔ lethal`
toggle has no member and needs a **second weapon entity**. That is the gap this card closes.

## 2. Steps

1. Copy the built cannon tune (`w602` clone from B-06's manifest) to
   `Working/Weapons/riot/<leaf>.wtunec`; set `damage = 0`, keep `impulse` and the two factors,
   set `fire_rate` 0.75, write the three angle members 7.5.
2. Read every written member back out of the private copy (no hand edits).
3. Author the second weapon entity: the riot variant clones the cannon entity and keeps the
   lethal copy untouched — the toggle is entity identity, because no tune member switches modes.
4. Attach the gel/tracer/casing effects on the entity side (T-17's tables), not in the tune.
5. Slot the riot entity on the second driver slot (`id:00568A74`) only if T-WP13's slot blocker is
   resolved; otherwise it stays a build-tree entity with no binding and the row says so.
6. Record in the receipt: which of the 11 weapons exists in lethal form and which in riot form.

## 3. Done (gate BX)

- `receipt.json` lists the riot tune and its 6 written values, each read back equal.
- The lethal tune is byte-identical to B-06's output (`sha256` unchanged).
- The riot entity exists in the tree and its effect nodes reference the three gel particles.
- The Q-1 prediction is written **before** the test: `damage 0` ⇒ no HP loss; `impulse` ⇒ target
  is pushed. If the engine kills at `damage 0`, the card is BROKEN and the row returns to
  `blocked`, not to `out`.

## 4. UNKNOWN

- Whether a **0-damage bullet** still applies impulse and hit reactions: only the pulse
  `gen_explosion_5m_nodamage.ftune` ships 0 damage, and it is an explosion object.
- Which autoaim member corresponds to the 7.5° cone.
- Whether the engine accepts `impulse` above the shipped bullet maximum once the factors clamp at 1.
- The riot sounds: both are missing objects, so the audio half cannot be carried from the grab.
- Whether NPCs have a stun/incapacitation state at all (outside the decoded vehicle damage model).

## 5. Revert

Delete `Working/Weapons/riot/`, the riot entity block, and the receipt entry; restore
`t_rows.py` `DISPO["T-WP10"]`/`["T-WP14"]` to `out` and re-run `t_rows.py build`. Nothing in this
card touches a game install.
