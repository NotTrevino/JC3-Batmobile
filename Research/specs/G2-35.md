# G2-35 backlog card — a triggered, non-damaging impulse with a target and a duration

Status: **todo** (opened 2026-11-06). Tracker row `GRAB_BACKLOG.md` §12. Ledger row `T-RA1`
(`blocked`): `Working/Translation/T3_COVERAGE.md`. Carrier survey: section 8/9 of
`tmp/freebuff/reports/carrier-ram-and-dodge.md`. The row names the closest JC3 systems and the gap the
build composes; "no JC3 system" is not its text.

**SUMMARY: this is a capability to be found, not a value to be translated.** BmAK's road assist,
dodge, shove and kill-flip are four names for one mechanism — an **event-triggered, non-damaging
impulse or torque with target selection and a duration**. JC3 has the ingredients (a yaw torque, a
push force, a fixed-axis jump punch, an explosion-fired `CForcePulse`) but no member joins them:
every one is continuous and none selects a target or carries a duration. Five layers were searched;
0 members. What this card has to produce is either that mechanism in a decoded file, or the proof
that only the native-hook route (R17) can supply it.

vehicle side**, and that is the whole of the difference its notes support:

| a pedestrian | the impact function reads `OtherVehicle` and only vehicle multipliers; BmAK's `Pawn` classes do carry `Health`/`HealthMax`/`Healer` (`DAMAGE_RULES.md` §5 SDK table), but **no decoded member or script routes a Batmobile contact into them** | AK's pedestrian outcome is UNKNOWN from the files. So the prediction the test carries is asymmetric on purpose: the **car** half is a number to measure
(20→150 pursuit, 120→540 battle, ×2.25 strafe, ×2.0 boost), the **pedestrian** half is an absence to
confirm, and a positive result there is new information rather than a failed test.
