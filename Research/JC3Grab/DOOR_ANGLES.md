# Every JC3 door part, its body and its hinge range in degrees

Generated by `Tools/scripts/jc3_door_angles.py` from the shipped bytes. `decoded` = read at the stated offset/file; `derived` = a rule over decoded inputs; `UNKNOWN` = no header or decoder pins it.

A door is three things (`Research/JC3Grab/DOORS.md`): a part whose `behavior` names a door behaviour, an entry in the parts module's `door_parts[]`, and a `hkpLimitedHingeConstraintData` in the vehicle's `.physicsc`. Since G3-10e the hinge's two angle limits decode at **+0x0FC (min)** and **+0x100 (max)**, radians - so the door's open range is exactly that pair.

This page follows the whole link across the fleet: **233 entities** in `INDEX.json`, **88 cars** whose parts module carries `door_parts[]`, **338 door rows**, **334 of them on a limited hinge**, over **1086 constraint records**.

Its `+0x0FC` reads 0.0 rad and its `+0x100` reads 1.3963 rad = 80 deg, so the door's open range is **0..80 deg**.

## 8. UNKNOWNs

- what drives a door: the module stores no open rate, animation curve or interaction for the part, so the hinge limits are the authored travel, not a measured swing (G2-15 §1/§3)
- the meaning of the constraint record's +0x0C u32: it is 0x7fffffff in every record of the fleet, decoded as a constant, but no Havok 2014 header in this project names the field
- why four door parts' body ids differ from their constraint record's body u32 (vintagesports door_f_l/door_f_r/hood, oldbus_trunk): the hinge is still resolved by the module's own constraint_index, and its pivot mirrors the opposite door with the same limits, but the two numberings are not one space and the body enumeration is not decoded
- the field names inside the 24 B constraint record: its stride and typed pointer are decoded by pattern (stride fits the array, +0x00 resolves to a *ConstraintData), not from a Havok header
- the frame the hinge axis and pivot are expressed in (body-local vs parent): the values are decoded as frame A row 0 and the translation, but comparing them to part centroids does not hold across the fleet, so that comparison is not used as an oracle
- which column of the hinge frame is the joint's free axis: G3-10e §2 leaves that UNKNOWN; the axis column reported here is frame A row 0, the convention G2-15 published
- the open range of the four door rows with constraint_index -1 (propeller and floatplane wing parts): there is no hinge at all, so no range is authored in the files
- vehicles outside INDEX.json's 233 jc_vehicles entity bundles: nothing else with a parts module was scanned, so a vehicle absent from the index is absent here
