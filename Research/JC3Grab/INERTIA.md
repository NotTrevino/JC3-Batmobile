## UNKNOWN

1. the scale that turns the quantised lanes into SI moments: no project decoder reads it, so only the shape of the moments is testable (all four oracle parts are scale-free). Candidate home: the fourth lane of each group (+0x16 11008..12544 and +0x1e 10368..13696), which stays in a narrow band in all 56 blocks and looks like an exponent or scale marker, but nothing in this tree documents it.
2. whether the third int16x4 group (+0x20..+0x27) is the three products or a principal axis: its triple norm is 1.3638..1.7321 in Q15, neither 1.0 (an axis) nor 0 (products near a principal frame), and two of its lanes are x-odd, which both readings allow.
3. whether the f32 at +0x2c is a volume, a density or an unused slot: it is 0 in 38 of 56 blocks and 0.0019..0.165 in the rest, with no monotone relation to the mass checked here.
4. the sign convention of the x-odd lanes (row vs column, right- vs left-handed). The mirror mask fixes *which* lanes are x-odd, not the handedness.
5. why box agreement fails: the hull is not a box (thin panels, curved doors), so the reference tensor itself is a model. A hull-exact tensor (tetrahedral decomposition of the polytope) would separate an encoding error from a reference-model error.

## What would settle it

Compute the inertia tensor of the hull itself (tetrahedral decomposition of the `hknpConvexPolytopeShape` vertices) instead of a solid box, and re-run the same scale-free comparison. If the payload is an inertia encoding, the grouped diagonal should match the hull tensor far more closely than it matches a box - and then the scale at `+0x1e`/`+0x16` becomes the only remaining unknown.
