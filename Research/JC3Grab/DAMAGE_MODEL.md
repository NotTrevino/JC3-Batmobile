# The JC3 damage model, and the predicted hits-to-kill table (G2-28)

Generated by `python Tools/scripts/damage_predict.py --write`; re-derived and compared
byte-for-byte by `--check`. Machine half: `DAMAGE_MODEL.json`.

**The short version.** The chain from a bullet to the health bar has seven steps and the
files give numbers for **four** of them and the *mechanism* for a fifth. The step that
decides how much of a hit actually reaches health - the composition of `*_armor`,
`*_armor_strength`, `max_health` and the root `health` float - is in no file the project
has decoded. It is therefore carried here as **three named models** with exact per-arm
numbers, and the three Q-1 probes below are the takes that can fail them. Nothing in this
file claims a composition the data does not state.

### 1.1 The three models this card computes with

| model | formula | what would make it the true one |
|---|---|---|
| **H0** | per-hit = D; hits = ceil(max_health / D) | armour is ignored entirely. This is the reference, not a claim: it is what the fleet looks like if `*_armor` only feeds deform and the part layer |
| **H1** | per-hit = D x (1 - strength/100); strength 100 = never | MODLOG 97's reading ("99/100 is not a full gate - it passes 1 % of every bullet"). **Counter-evidence:** test-077 shipped exactly strength 100 and still failed (MODLOG 98, symptom unrecorded) |
| **H2** | D <= strength -> absorbed; else ceil((armor + HP)/D) | the two fields are an absorb-and-block pair: `*_armor` = pool, `_strength` = per-hit threshold. The fleet's `bullet_armor` 5000 maximum and the medium tank's 5000 @ 99 fit a 5,000-point pool; the donor's 0 @ 0 fits "no armour at all" |

**Measured, and it matters.** The build's scalars differ from the **medium tank's** in
6 values: `deform_scale` 1 -> 0, `impact_armor` 0 -> 5000, `impact_armor_strength` 0 -> 100, `impact_damage_factor` 1 -> 0, `melee_armor` 0 -> 5000, `melee_armor_strength` 0 -> 100.
Against the **donor** it differs in 23 values. So the
build is the tank's profile with the impact and melee pairs raised to 5000 @ 100 and the
two off-switches set - not a translation of the donor, which is 700 HP with every channel
at 0 @ 0 (everything above is counted, not asserted: `DAMAGE_MODEL.json` carries the six
field-by-field diffs under `diffs`).

The part table is a third, separate thing. The same 24 `Land_*` names appear in all
three, but the tank's numbers differ from the donor's in
24 values, while the **build's part table is the
donor's with exactly 1 change**:
`Land_Wheel.instantly_damaged_by_bullets` **1 -> 0** (donor -> build). That single flag is
the only ARM-4-shaped edit in the live build: a bullet no longer **instantly** damages
the `Land_Wheel` type. It is not on its own proof of ARM-4 - the wheel keeps
`initial_health` 1020 and `loosen_health` 100, so sustained fire can still take it to
the damaged state over time; that is Q-1's business, not this file's.
It is the only part-level damage edit in the build, and it is in the donor's table, not the
tank's - a fact no earlier document states.

Vehicle arms come from `WEAPONS.jsonl`; hand arms are re-decoded from the archives, because
G2-10 only covered `06_vehicle/`. `per shot vs vehicle` = damage x vehicle_damage_factor x
pellets - the multiplication is **derived** from the two field names, not read.

| `assault_rifle` | Rico's assault rifle (w111_UP_39) | `bullet_weapon` | 32 | 1 | 1 | 32 | - | bullet |
| `machine_gun` | Rico's machine gun (w131_Vdova_89) | `bullet_weapon` | 56 | 0.5 | 1 | 28 | - | bullet |

| `grenade` | Rico's grenade (w301) | `grenade_weapon` | 750 | 1.35 | 1 | 1012.5 | 700 in 8 m | explosion |
| `tank_cannon` | tank cannon w602_mediumtank_canon | `bullet_weapon` | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1500 in 8 m | explosion |
| `heli_missile` | heli missile w601_helicopter_missile | `missile_weapon` | 1500 | 1 | 1 | 1500 | - | bullet |
| `heli_mg` | heli MG w604_helicopter_mgun | `bullet_weapon` | 250 | 0.1 | 1 | 25 | - | bullet |

`w604` also ships an impact pulse (`w604_helicopter_mgun_impact.ftunec`, PlayerDamage 25 in 1.5 m) as the `CForcePulse` of the
two attack helicopters (G2-10), **not** as a field of the weapon; whether it fires on
every bullet hit is UNKNOWN, so the table uses the direct 25 and notes 50 as the
alternative on those two helicopters only.

Two facts about the arms that the card's list does not expect:

1. **The tank cannon's `bullet_weapon` damage is 1.0** (player tune) - a tank shell does its
   work through the **1500 blast in 8 m**, not through the bullet. `w602`'s AI tune is 20 x
   10 = 200, which is the AI-vs-vehicle path.
2. **The shotgun fires 8 pellets of 90 x 0.2** = 144 per trigger pull, more per hit than any
   other small arm by 4.5x - which is why the ARM-1 gate cannot be set from the pistol's
   number alone.

root pool is what takes the hit.

Rows whose channel is `unknown` (a missile direct hit and the grenade's thrown damage:
no armour channel in `VehicleDamage` is named for either) are computed with the
no-armour value, so their count is a **lower bound** on hits, not a prediction.)

Read the three columns as a bracket, not a prediction: the true count for each arm is one
of them, and Q1-DM-A/B/C pick which. What is **not** model-dependent:

- the donor (700 HP, every channel 0) takes **the same count under all three models**,
  because every model agrees when armour is 0; only the shotgun (144) and the blasts take
  more than one hit;
- the medium tank and the build are effectively bullet-proof under H1 and H2 (6,667 pistol
  rounds, or `never`) and merely tough under H0 (67) - the models disagree by two orders of
  magnitude on exactly the channel ARM-1 is about;
- a tank shell (1500 in 8 m) is 2 hits on 2000 HP under H0/H2 and 3 under H1, so the
  explosion channel is where the models can be told apart at a small, testable count.

## 5. What values would give ARM-1 and ARM-2

The BmAK side is decoded (`DAMAGE_RULES.json`, G1-14): `Pawn.Health` **1200**, the heavy
cannon projectile `Damage` **50**, missile 250, and `ArmourModeDamageReduction`
**0** - so the BmAK ratio is
**1200 / 50 = 24 heavy-cannon hits** (or 1200 / 250 = 5 missile hits).

| requirement | what it needs | inside the shipped domain? |
|---|---|---|
| **ARM-1** small arms do not move the bar | H1: `bullet_armor_strength` 100 | **no** - the fleet maximum is 99, and test-077 shipped 100 and failed |
| | H2: `bullet_armor_strength` >= 144 | **no** - 99 is the maximum, and the shotgun delivers 144 per pull |
| | either: `invulnerable` 1 on the `CCar` root | **yes** (4 bytes at `epe+1167`) but it is whole-vehicle |
| **ARM-2** heavy weapons destroy it | H2: explosion strength below the shell damage (1500) - the build ships 50 | **yes**, already true |
| | H1: strength below 100 so the shell still passes | **yes**, already true |
| **ARM-2 at the BmAK ratio** (24 shell hits) | `max_health` = 24 x 1500 = 36,000 | **no** - the fleet range is 500..4000, i.e. 3 hits under H0/H2 and 6 under H1 at the maximum |

**The consequence for the ledger.** ARM-1 and ARM-2 cannot both be expressed by
`invulnerable` (it is whole-vehicle), and the only per-channel split the files offer is the
**bullet vs bullet_heavy** pair - which is exactly how the medium tank is built (bullet
5000 @ 99, bullet_heavy 5 @ 20). Whether a given weapon lands in `bullet` or
`bullet_heavy` is UNKNOWN (step 3), so T-14 cannot write those rows from the files alone:
it needs either a `bullet_heavy` carrier found in G2-11's weapon components, or the Q-1
probe below. Writing `bullet_armor_strength` 100 was tried once (test-077) and failed.

The root `health` float is **100.0 on all 233 roots**, so a scale other than 100 has no
precedent in the fleet and no range in the files: for hits-to-kill it is UNKNOWN whether
it scales `max_health` at all. The table above assumes it is a 1.0 multiplier (all three
targets ship 100.0).

## 6. Q-1 damage probes (exact expected values)

### `Q1-DM-A` - is the armour strength a percentage gate (H1) or a per-hit threshold (H2)?

- vehicle: `build_test-091`, arm: `tank_cannon`, channel: `explosion`
- setup: one tank shell into the Batmobile's body, at the centre of the 8 m blast
- **H0**: 2 shells
- **H1**: 3 shells
- **H2**: 2 shells
- why: strength 50 makes H1 halve the 1500 blast; H2 lets it through because 1500 > 50. The explosion channel is the only one where the two models disagree at a testable count

### `Q1-DM-B` - does the bullet gate at strength 99 leak 1 % (H1) or absorb everything up to strength (H2)?

- vehicle: `build_test-091`, arm: `pistol`, channel: `bullet`
- setup: 200 pistol rounds at the body, health read before and after
- **H0**: 200 rounds = 6,000 damage = destroyed
- **H1**: 200 rounds x 0.3 = 60 damage, bar barely moves, no destruction
- **H2**: 0 damage, bar unchanged
- why: H0 and H1/H2 are separated by 3 orders of magnitude; H1 vs H2 is 60 HP vs 0 HP on a 2000 HP bar (3 %) - readable only if the HUD shows a number. If test-077's failure was small-arms damage, H1 is already falsified for this channel

### `Q1-DM-C` - does an emptied armour pool take the hit instead of health (H2 pool half)?

- vehicle: `medium_tank`, arm: `tank_cannon`, channel: `explosion`
- setup: two tank shells into a stock medium tank (explosion_armor 250). Not the donor: its pool is 0, so H2 collapses to H0 there and the pool half cannot be seen
- **H0**: 2 shells
- **H1**: 3 shells
- **H2**: 2 shells (250 absorbed, then 1750 of 2000)
- why: the pool half of H2 is invisible on the donor (armour 0) and only shows on a vehicle with a non-zero pool

## 7. Fleet evidence for the gate question

`233` `VehicleDamage` modules. Distinct `armor @ strength` pairs per channel
(vehicles):

| channel | pairs |
|---|---|
| `bullet` | `0@0` x183, `100@50` x18, `5000@99` x13, `20@60` x9, `2000@99` x6, `100@75` x4 |
| `bullet_heavy` | `0@0` x189, `100@30` x18, `5@20` x14, `5@40` x9, `100@50` x3 |
| `explosion` | `0@0` x193, `100@30` x14, `100@100` x13, `250@50` x13 |
| `impact` | `0@0` x220, `5@100` x13 |
| `fire` | `0@0` x233 |
| `melee` | `0@0` x233 |
| `electricity` | `0@0` x192, `-1@100` x41 |
| `grapple_pull` | `0@0` x192, `-1@100` x41 |

The `bullet` channel is the only one where a large minority sits at the maximum (99) while
most sit at 0; `impact`, `melee`, `electricity` and `grapple_pull` cluster at their
maximums or at `-1`; `explosion` spreads across 0..100. That is consistent with `bullet`
being the one gate the designers actually tuned per vehicle - and with `bullet_heavy` being
the second, at much lower values.

## 8. UNKNOWNs, stated

- **the composition rule itself** (step 4/6) - see the three models; no file states it.
- **part -> damage type for all three vehicles** (step 1): every `*_vehicle_parts.vmodc`
  except the monorail flatbed fails ConvertAdf. So the part layer cannot be predicted at
  all, and every hits-to-kill number here is "whole vehicle takes every hit".
- **weapon -> armour channel** (step 3): no tune carries a damage-type or heavy flag.
- **root `health` float semantics** (step 6): all 233 roots are 100.0.
- **`explosion_threshold` semantics** (step 7).
- **which pistol/rifle etc. Rico starts with**: the table uses one named variant per class
  (`w011`, `w111`, `w131`, `w151`, `w141`, `w301`); the class spans 5 pistol / 4 AR / 2 MG /
  4 shotgun / 7 RPG variants in the index, and no file names a default loadout.
- **the BmAK incoming-damage numbers**: `GetDamageMod` is native (G1-14 §7), so the BmAK
  side of the ratio is the projectile `Damage`, not the health loss a player sees.
