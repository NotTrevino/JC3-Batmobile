## 1. Answer

**The brief's premise has to be corrected: in JC3 the paint parameters are not in
`cb0` at all.**  Every one of the 12 shaders of the family binds them to `cb2`, `cb6`
and `cb7` (and `RBIInfo` to `cb8`), so a `cb0[n]` search is looking in the wrong buffer.

With the bind numbers known, the slots *are* nameable from files on disk, because the
shipped `.rbm` `CarPaintMM` block is the constant-buffer payload itself:

| cbuffer | bind | size | source of the numbers | slots named |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|
| `CarPaintStaticMaterialParams` | `cb2` | 316 | 18 float4 + 7 floats, exactly the header's member list | 25 |
| `CarPaintDynamicMaterialParams` | `cb6` | 76 | 4 float4 + 3 floats | 7 |
| `CarPaintDynamicObjectParams` | `cb7` | 20 | 1 float4 + 1 float | 2 |

The Arkham 6,943 uniform records yield **0** named slots this way: an entry is 32 B -
class FName, parameter FName, RGBA - with no offset, slot or bind field anywhere in the
9553 entries, and none of the 14 parameter names of the one fully decoded record matches a
JC3 paint member (0 matches).  They are name-to-value lists for Arkham's shaders; naming
a JC3 slot from them would need the `RDEF` chunk G3-04d showed absent from all 40,299
containers.

## 4. `CarPaintDynamicMaterialParams` - the dynamic block (bind `cb6`)

| slot | offset | size | member | `.rbm` dynamic field |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| `cb6[0]` | `0` | 16 | `m_TintColorR` | float4[0] @ payload +0x145 |
| `cb6[1]` | `16` | 16 | `m_TintColorG` | float4[1] @ payload +0x155 |
| `cb6[2]` | `32` | 16 | `m_TintColorB` | float4[2] @ payload +0x165 |
| `cb6[3]` | `48` | 16 | `_unused` | float4[3] @ payload +0x175 |
| `cb6[4].x` | `64` | 4 | `m_SpecularGlossOverride` | float[0] @ payload +0x185 |
| `cb6[4].y` | `68` | 4 | `m_MetallicOverride` | float[1] @ payload +0x189 |
| `cb6[4].z` | `72` | 4 | `m_ClearCoatOverride` | float[2] @ payload +0x18d |

The 76-byte size is the stride the engine uploads: `imul ..., 0x4c` in the synced
constant-data update at `0x140259f00`, whose CPU-side fill `0x140289a80` copies source
`+0x00/+0x10/+0x20` into the parity copy (G3-09's `TINT_WRITE.md`, cited, not re-derived
here).  That is the fill order: float4 0..2 of the block are `m_TintColorR/G/B`.

| ambiguous | 0 | nothing was guessed, so nothing is ambiguous |
| unnameable | **6943** | every record |

Why: the records are Arkham's, keyed by material name and ordered by parameter name -
the engine that consumes them assigns the slots, and the chunk that records that
assignment (`RDEF`) is absent.  JC3 does not need those records: its equivalent
name->offset table is the reflection header set, present on disk and tested above.

## 8. UNKNOWN

- **Which colour member is albedo.** `m_DiffuseWrap` and `ModelDiffuseColor` are both
  decoded `float4`s; the multiply chain is in the SHEX, which is not on disk.
- **Whether the palette RGB lands in `m_TintColorR/G/B` verbatim.** G3-09 measured that
  no asset names the palette; TINT_WRITE follows the uploader to the block's `+0x168`
  fields but not to the palette record.
- **The `.epe` parameter lists.** The brief named them as an input, but the paint constants
  are not there: `COLOUR.md` measured that no `.rbm`, `.epe` or `.vmodc` of the entity names
  the palette (a scan of all 566 inner files for `palette` hits 1 file - the palette module
  itself), and the `.rbm` block already carries the header's 18+7 layout in order.  Whether
  any `.epe` holds shader constants at all is UNKNOWN here.
