# JC3 vehicle weapon catalogue (G2-10)

Generated by `Tools/scripts/jc3weapons.py` from the archives; `--check` re-reads them and
compares. One row per weapon id in `WEAPONS.jsonl`, with every decoded member of every
authoritative tune; this file is the reading of those rows.

Three ADF v4 tune classes carry the fleet's weapons:

| class | what it is | instances decoded |
|---|---|---|
| `WeaponTuning` | the weapon: fire rate, burst, magazine, damage, recoil, scatter, projectile, homing, AI priorities, reticle | 22 ids |
| `AutoAim` | sticky-aim / lock-on block | 3 ids + 11 shared tunes |
| `ForcePulseSettings` | the blast: player/AI damage, radius, falloff | 9 ids |

## 1. Where the tunes live

`INDEX.jsonl` has **81,952** rows; **61** of them sit under `editor/entities/jc_weapons/06_vehicle/` (22 of the 25 ids have their own directory, 14 of those carry an `.ee`). The other ids ship as loose patch `.wtunec` overrides and their base copy lives inside the carrier `.ee`. 67 `.ee` bundles were opened (14 weapon bundles + 53 armed vehicles).

Two things the card expected that are not there, recorded so nobody re-litigates them:

* **No `editor/entities/jc_explosions/` tree.** The weapon blasts are `.ftunec` files inside
  `.ee` bundles; the whole game has exactly two standalone `.ftunec` archive entries, neither
  a weapon (`gen_explosion_0.5m_nodamage`, `structures/barrel_explosion`).
* **The 3 `+_impact` ids are blast tunes, not weapons.** Each is the `SettingsFile` of a
  `CForcePulse` object on the vehicles that fire the matching gun (`w604_helicopter_mgun_impact` on 3 `v2601` + 9 `v2602`/`v2604` helicopters per the census).

**Overheat is absent, and that is a file answer.** No member of any decoded class names
heat, cooling or temperature; the single cooldown-shaped member is 
`burst_cooldown`, and no weapon tunes a temperature 
anywhere. JC3 weapons therefore have no heat model at all: a JC3 Vulcan fires until its
ammo runs out, and BmAK's `HeatUpPerSecond` / `OverheatOffValue` (grab record) has no JC3
counterpart to copy - a heat-carrying Batmobile Vulcan is a BmAK-side behaviour, and the
Vulcan's bloom curve is the JC3-shaped half.

Members that matched anyway, for the record: `burst_cooldown`.
