Whether they are min/max of a local AABB or a query vector is not proven by any instruction in the chain: no min/max compare exists. Where it would be found: the callee 0x141516b40's first uses of its 4th stack argument.
- **whether node+0x150 and node+0x158 are the same world object** — both are used identically (slot array at +0x60, stride 128, vtbl call [rax+0x20] compared to 8) but no instruction compares or copies the two pointers. Where it would be found: a setter of node+0x150/+0x158.
- **where the runtime record's int16 handles (+0x100/+0x102) are written** — the record is read at 0x140201e35/0x140201e55 and consumed for the slot lookup, but no instruction in the reviewed chunk chains writes them, so the transfer named in the document's UNKNOWN 5 is still open. Where it would be found: writers of word ptr [x+0x100]/[x+0x102] where x = [part+0x140].
