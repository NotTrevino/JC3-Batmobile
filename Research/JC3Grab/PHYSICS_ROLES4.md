## 1. The cinfo record, fleet-wide (decoded)

24 bytes per constraint, `hknpPhysicsSystemData +0x50`, six words:

| word | offset | content | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-1 | +0x00..+0x07 | `constraintData*` packfile fixup | resolves to the constraint object in every record |
| 2 | +0x04 | body id A | always 0 in 494/494 records |
| 3 | +0x08 | body id B | always < the file's body count (494/494) |
| 4 | +0x0C | 0x7FFFFFFF | constant |
| 5-6 | +0x10..+0x17 | 0 | constant |

That is the reading G3-10c made, and it leaves the partner side open: either the constant 0 at +0x04 is a flag and the record holds *one* body, or it is body id A and +0x08 is body id B. Section 2 tests the second reading instead of assuming it.

## 3. Why the pivots cannot separate A from B (decoded)

Each `hkpConstraintData` holds two 3x4 frames (A and B). In the donor **11/11** have bit-identical translations, the two rotations agree to within 0.05 deg and every delta is a proper rotation (det 1); the frame counts per constraint are 2, 3. The A/B pair is therefore the same frame written twice: the constraintData gives the joint's position and axis but says nothing about which body is A. That is why section 2 must use the hull test rather than a pivot comparison.

## 4. The 24 packed bytes: every candidate reading, scored (UNKNOWN)

The link is decoded: `polytope + 0x20` -> `hkRefCountedProperties + 0x10` -> `hknpShapeMassProperties`; **2011** hulls across 46 files carry a mass block. Mass is the IEEE float at `+0x28`. The 24 bytes at `+0x10..+0x27` are the packed properties.

Oracle: for a box of the hull's AABB and its own mass,

```
Ixx = m (ey^2 + ez^2)/12     Iyy = m (ex^2 + ez^2)/12     Izz = m (ex^2 + ey^2)/12
```

A packed reading must give a *constant* scale inside a body (`I_i / value_i` equal on all three axes). Spread = max/min of those ratios, median over the hulls; a decode needs ~1.0:

| reading | best triple | median spread | n | decodes? |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 6 x IEEE f32 | [0, 1, 2] | 12541147323961291309056.000 | 194 | no |
| 12 x int16 raw | [4, 5, 6] | 1.327 | 1955 | no |
| 12 x int16 / 16384 | [4, 5, 6] | 1.327 | 1955 | no |
| 12 x int16 / 32767 | [4, 5, 6] | 1.327 | 1955 | no |
| 12 x u16 raw | [4, 5, 6] | 1.327 | 1955 | no |
| 12 x IEEE half | [3, 4, 5] | 6116.134 | 1890 | no |

No reading decodes. The bar is a per-axis agreement within 15% of one scale (spread <= 1.15); the best candidate is still 1.33, i.e. ~33% disagreement, which is the same order as the AABB-vs-hull shape error of the oracle itself. The test therefore cannot certify any reading: a true packed inertia could sit in that band and so could noise.
