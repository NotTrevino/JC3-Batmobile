## VERDICT

**The three load-bearing claims hold, one supporting claim does not, and two smaller
statements in the card are wrong.**  Every branch, target, stride and field the card
quotes in the cinfo loop is byte-exact, the six invalid-body branches do all land on
`0x141569fc1` (`add rbx, 0x18`), and nothing in the loop writes to the record or calls anything
but the add, so a sentinel record is dropped.  The sentinel the loop tests is byte-
identical to the value stored in 494/494 fleet records.

But the card's completeness claim - "only one function tests both body offsets", used to
argue that no other consumer can repair an invalid body - is **false**: this review finds
**2** such functions (`0x141569d00-0x141569ff0`, `0x1415e6030-0x1415e6651`).
The second one also walks 0x18-byte records and tests `+0xc` then `+8` against
`0x7fffffff`; its sentinel branches go to an error-report block, not to a body, so the
card's *conclusion* survives, but the evidence sentence and the "exactly one place"
paragraph must be corrected.  Two further inaccuracies: the `hknpWorldCinfo` member table
is presented as "exactly these members" while the registry record carries 25, and the two
name strings are listed against the wrong addresses.

## 8. What this review did not check

- Whether the object the walker passes in `rcx` is a `hknpWorld` or a
  `hknpPhysicsSystem`: the card calls `rsi` the *system* in section 2 and the *world* in
  section 3, and names `0x1414e7200` `hknpWorld::addConstraint` from its source strings alone.
- Whether `[rsi+0x2c] & 0x3fffffff` is a capacity and bits 0..1 of the body's `+0x40`
  byte mean "live": both are readings of the guard, not decoded semantics. The branch
  *targets* do not depend on either reading.
- Whether a loader rewrites `+0xc` on its way into memory; if it did, the derived 494/494
  rejection count would change. No writer was searched here.
- Who dispatches the walker (the card's UNKNOWN 1) and what the second consumer's
  records are (its array is at `+0x50`, not `+0x140`); neither affects the guard.
