# The JC3 vehicle light system (G2-33)

One place for the LGT-1/LGT-2 evidence: the light-entry member list, the presets, how a
light binds to a part (`has_lights`), the `CarLight` block format, what a custom light
without a preset needs, and the wheel-arch light recipe with a source for every value.
Generated by `Tools/scripts/jc3lights.py --write` from the decoded caches and tables;
`--check` rebuilds both files. No archive is opened and no game is launched.

## 3. Presets: what they do and do not decide

- **393 distinct `preset_id` hashes** are used across the fleet.
- **17 shipped entries use `preset_id = 0`** - e.g. the modernbus brake lights and the police siren - so a light does not need a preset to ship.
- **34 presets appear with more than one value tuple** across the vehicles that use them (top: `1271816577` x17, `1358616627` x16, `3048938132` x15, `1435191809` x14), so the preset does **not** fix the numbers: the entry's own members are what the
  data carries, and an entry that names a preset still ships its own `range`, `angle`,
  `multiplier` and colour.
- What the engine does with the preset (override the entry, or only pick the sound/
  behaviour) is code: `UNKNOWN`.

## 4. How a light binds to a part (`has_lights`)

The binding is the triple (`part`, `part_index`, `deformable_transform`) on the module entry, resolved against the vehicle's parts module. `has_lights` is a per-part flag in
that module (2,073 of 11,643 parts fleet-wide carry it, 1,323 of them `Land_Light`); the `.epe` does not carry it.

| check | entries |
|---|---|
| light entries | 3,217 |
| `part_index` not in the parts module | 3 |
| `part_index` resolves to a **different** part name | 27 |
| part resolves but `has_lights = 0` | 6 |
| `has_lights = 1` parts with **no** light entry | 13 |

The exceptions, so nobody re-derives them:

- `v2601_helicopter_urga_heavyassault_debug` part 39 (`heavyassault_wing_light_r`): part_index not in the module
- `v2601_helicopter_urga_heavyassault_military_01` part 39 (`heavyassault_wing_light_r`): part_index not in the module
- `v2601_helicopter_urga_heavyassault_rebel_01` part 39 (`heavyassault_wing_light_r`): part_index not in the module
- `v2601_helicopter_urga_heavyassault_debug` part 37 (`heavyassault_wing_light_l`): module has heavyassault_missile_f_r
- `v2601_helicopter_urga_heavyassault_debug` part 35 (`heavyassault_tail_light`): module has heavyassault_missile_f_l
- `v2601_helicopter_urga_heavyassault_military_01` part 37 (`heavyassault_wing_light_l`): module has heavyassault_missile_f_r
- `v0202_car_capstone_bavariumtank_debug` part 24 (`bavariumtank_turret`): has_lights = 0
- `v0202_car_capstone_bavariumtank_debug` part 24 (`bavariumtank_turret`): has_lights = 0
- `v0202_car_capstone_bavariumtank_military_01` part 24 (`bavariumtank_turret`): has_lights = 0

So `has_lights` is a **flag the artist sets, not a gate the loader enforces**: entries
exist on parts with it clear (6 of them), and 13 of the 2,073 lit parts carry no
entry at all. Entries outnumber lit parts because **one part hosts several lamps** -
the donor's left tail-light part alone carries a `TailLight`, a `LeftSignal` and a
`Reverse` entry. The flag is still the right one for a new light part: every vanilla
light part has it set.

Donor (`v0300_car_kensington_sportsmuscle_civilian_01`): 42 parts, 12 with `has_lights = 1`, 10 of them `Land_Light`; the rest are `Land_Body` x1, `Land_Generic` x1.

## 5. The `CarLight` block

| item | value |
|---|---|
| blocks in the fleet | 11,367 (one per light mesh) |
| meshes / vehicles carrying one | 11,367 / 220 |
| vertex stride | 12 B x11,367 |
| index width | 2 B x11,367 |
| material slots | 6 x11,367 |

Layout (solved from the files, MODLOG.md §17 ('Window and CarLight solved from the files'); solver Tools/scripts/_probe_layout.py, round-trip 190/193 blocks byte-identical, 0 mismatched): static header **1085 B**, 16 B gap, buffers `[12, 24, 2]` (vertex buffer, second stream, uint16 indices).
Parameters: **UNKNOWN - the material/emissive parameter bytes are not decoded (MODLOG §102d), so a CarLight block can be copied byte-exact but not authored from numbers**.

What that means for a build: a light mesh is `CarLight` (emitter geometry) + `Window`
(lens glass). Copying a vanilla light part's blocks and clipping our own geometry into
them is safe (byte-exact round-trip proven); changing the emissive numbers is not,
because no decoded field says which parameter is the colour.

## 6. What a custom light without a preset needs

1. **A part to hang on.** The entry's `part_index` must exist in the parts module and
   its name must match `part`, or the binding is broken (see section 4). `has_lights = 1`
   on that part, and `Land_Light` as its damage type is the vanilla convention.
2. **An anchor in that part's rig.** `deformable_transform` is a node chain
   (`|sportsmuscle_LOD1|wheel_f_l|Wheel_Frnt_Left_Disc|Wheel_Frnt_Left_Disc_LIG`); the
   node is not in the RBM, not in the parts `bones` array and not in the deform table
   (LGT1_FINDINGS §5), so the part needs the node - either a rigged part or a dedicated
   light part placed at the socket. Which of those resolves is the §112 probe.
3. **All 25 members.** Every entry carries its own values (section 3), so a light with
   `preset_id = 0` is fully described by its row - that is what the 17 shipped zero-preset
   entries do.
4. **An emitter mesh.** A `CarLight` block in the part's `lod1.rbm` (plus `Window` for
   the lens) - the assembled Batmobile currently has none (LGT1_FINDINGS §4).

## 7. The wheel-arch light recipe

Source: `Playable_Batmobile_SF` / `LWheelLight` (VEHLT_HeadLight, material VEHLTMAT_Special3), bone `Wheel_Frnt_Left_Disc`, socket at [-0.349729, 50.689182, 26.0] (BmAK cm).
Its `SpotLightComponent` ships `LightColor` [160, 180, 255, 0], `Radius` 0.0,
`OuterConeAngle` 0.0, `Brightness` 0.0 - i.e. **the BmAK
component itself is unlit** (radius, cone and brightness 0, colour alpha 0), so the
shape of the lamp is ours to choose and only the colour is decodable from it.

The same socket also carries 1 `VEHLT_BattleModeLight`
entry (VEHLT_BattleModeLight -> HeadLight) - that is LGT-2's battle-mode spotlight, a second entry on this recipe's part
with the same 25 members and no extra machinery.

### 7c. Why the vanilla wheel part cannot host it as-is

`sportsmuscle_wheel_f_l` ships `has_lights = 0` and its meshes are `CarPaintMM` only (all
four wheel parts, every LOD): there is no `CarLight` block to carry the glow and no
anchor node. That is why the recipe adds a part rather than editing the wheel's entry.

## 8. What the files do not say (UNKNOWN)

- **Which list the renderer reads** - the module entries (part-bound) or the `.epe`
  `SVehicleLight` objects (3,972, preset-named, no part): `UNKNOWN`; the
  counts differ and no file joins them.
- **`preset_id` semantics** - whether the preset overrides the entry's members: `UNKNOWN`
  (the data only shows that entries with one preset carry different numbers).
- **`CarLight` material parameters** - which bytes are the emissive colour/intensity:
  `UNKNOWN` (MODLOG §102d). Copy blocks, never author them.
- **Range/angle units** - the vanilla values look like metres and degrees and the BmAK
  cone is in degrees, but no file states the unit: the recipe copies shipped numbers
  instead of converting, and only the colour is converted (sRGB / 255).
- **Whether `preset_id = 0` means "no preset"** or is simply a hash that no preset uses:
  `UNKNOWN`; it is what 17 shipped lights carry.
- **Anchor resolution** - whether a node chain in `deformable_transform` resolves through
  the parts module: the §112 probe (one part, one install, one night look).
- **Any light/preset table outside the vehicles**: 588 archive paths mention 'light' outside
  `jc_vehicles` and **0** is a vehicle light-preset table - they are
  environment models and localisation. So the preset name space is per-vehicle data, not
  a global table (at least in the decoded index).
