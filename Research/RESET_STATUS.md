## How a verdict is reached

* **RESOLVED** — every core card's report says `done`, no core card is under a
  `needs rework` review, and every evidence probe holds.
* **PARTIAL** — at least one evidence probe holds, or at least one core card is
  done/partial (something is decoded, the item's own question is not fully answered).
* **OPEN** — neither: nothing in the G3 outputs answers it.

Labels used below: **decoded** = a value read from a named file at a named offset/export by
the cited card; **derived** = a rule over decoded values; **UNKNOWN** = not decoded, with
what was tried. A card's own STATUS line is quoted; an independent review verdict is shown
next to it where one exists.
