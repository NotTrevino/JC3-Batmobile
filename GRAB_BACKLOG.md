## T — Translation

Definition: a **row-per-property table** where every row is falsifiable.

```
T-1  BmAK source (file, property or curve, offset) 
     → JC3 target (entity, module, member)
     → transform (identity | linear(scale, offset) | curve fit | categorical map)
     → evidence (why this transform: units, both endpoints' values)
     → confidence (decoded | derived | assumed)
     → verification (what measurement confirms it, or "geometry-only")
```
