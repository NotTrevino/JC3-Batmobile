# BmAK skins and variants -> JC3 routes (D-5)

**Scope.** Every look Arkham Knight's Batmobile can show: the standard (V2), MK2, Jokerfied and
Funhouse packages, the damaged story states (`Batmobile_V3_Destroyed`, `Batmobile_V2_Fire`), the
upgrade-level visuals (`Missile_Upgrade`, `Nimbus_Power_Cell`, `Stagg_Battery`, the `_Std_1..6`
stage packages), and the Joker chapter masks. Read against D-5 and what `Builds/bm-RC2` ships.

**First, a path correction.** `Working/Appearance/VARIANTS.md` **does not exist**. The variant
dataset is `Research/BatmobileGrab/VARIANTS.md` (G1-20, generated by `grab_variants.py`), and the
plan that consumes it is `Working/Appearance/APPEARANCE_PLAN.md` §6. Everything below is from
those two plus `MESHES.md`, `MATERIALS.md`, `SLOTS.md`, `TEXTURES.md`.

**D-5, decoded.** [decoded: `GRAB_BACKLOG.md` §11.]

> **D-5 — Skins (MK2, Jokerfied) as extra drop entries, palette tints, or not at all.**
> Data: G1-20. **Resolved 2026-10-28 (user): skins as extra Rebel Drop entries** (MK2,
> Jokerfied, …). Blocks **APP-8**.

And the plan's own consequence (`APPEARANCE_PLAN.md` §6): *"Each skin is its own drop entry, so
each one carries its own body RBM, its own material blocks and its own bake; this plan is written
for the reference `Batmobile_V2` set and re-applies to a skin's material set unchanged, because
the actions are per factor name, not per material."*

**Verdict.**

* **BmAK holds four complete vehicles plus three damage states and four upgrade visuals** - 11
  looks in all (§1).
* **One of them is carried: V2.** `bm-RC2`'s receipt says `variants: "one tree (D-2 = one hybrid
  car)"`, so D-5 is recorded and **not yet built** - there is no skin entry in the release.
* **The upgrade visuals are half-carried**: `Missile_Upgrade_D/N`, `Power_Cell_INT_D` +
  `Nimbus_Power_Cell_N`, `Stagg_Battery_01_D/N` ship as `.ddsc` in `build_parts`, but every `_S`
  in that set was dropped by D3 and no `_GLOW` shipped.
* **The skins are not palette tints and cannot become them**: V2 vs Joker differs by 61 materials,
  54 textures and 40 bones; MK2 replaces the body and wheel materials outright.
* **The damaged states have no material route at all** - they are separate meshes, and JC3's
  closest mechanism is the mesh swap the build already uses for wheels.
* **7 of 11 looks have no carrier today; 4 of those need a new drop entry, 3 a mesh/part swap.**

## 4. Why a palette tint will not do (the D-5 alternatives, closed)

| alternative | why it fails on the data |
|---|---|
| palette tint | V2→Joker changes **61 materials and 54 textures**, adds 40 bones and a Jokerfied mesh; a tint changes neither geometry nor the FX material set |
| one car, material swap at runtime | JC3 has no runtime material-set switch that would also swap the mesh and the 244-bone hierarchy |
| "not at all" | excluded by D-5's ruling |

**But a skin is cheap in the one place it matters:** `SimulationModel` is byte-identical across all
four packages (2,328 B, 37 tags, **0 differing tags**), and every shared weapon object has **0
differing fields** (`VARIANTS.md` §5, §7). So a new drop entry needs no new physics and no new
weapon tunes - only meshes, materials and textures.

## 5. Closest system, gap, build action (STAGES.md)

| gap | closest JC3 system | build action |
|---|---|---|
| a second look for the same car | **a second Rebel Drop entry** (D-5's ruling) with its own `.rbm` + material blocks | B-App-2: MK2 entry |
| a story-state body | **mesh swap** (`mshswap`), the mechanism the wheel already uses in RC2 | B-App-3: destroyed swap at 0 health |
| a burning car | JC3's burn effects + `Burnout` material path | B-App-4: fire shell + effect at `burn_threshold` |
| upgrade visuals | **texture swap** on the existing slot blocks (16, 17) | B-App-5: bind `_S` or bake it |
| per-pixel roughness in any of them | `s2` PropertyMap channel packing (channel map undecoded) | the same 4-block probe named in the cockpit report |
| entity budget | R9 texture wall **7,065 KB per entity** | per-skin totals **not measured** - a skin may not fit |

**The number to plan against:** a skin entry is not free. Joker adds 26 materials and 31 textures
to a 78/92 baseline; if the 7,065 KB wall is per entity, each skin needs its own budget
measurement before it can be promised.
