   entity leaves, **158/233** vehicles. The split matters more than the total: **0 of 86** base
   `spawn_vehicle_defs.bin` records are unregistered, while **163 of 169** debug records

   of those (`v0806_car_na_sportmech_01`, the sport mech) is playable in retail. A retail-playable
   entity with no record ⇒ a missing record does not stop a load.
3. **A path reference is expressible for a new leaf.** Spawn records hold the entity as a full path
   string (base table uses backslashes, DLC tables forward slashes); 101 of the 196 distinct spawn
   leaves already have no registry record.

   `.epe` inside — **plus one reference** (spawn record or drop item), name/icon optional. The
   `.toc` is optional: the shipped mod ships 20 `.ee` with **0** `.toc`, and our donor `.toc` has
