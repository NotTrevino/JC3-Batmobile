## UNKNOWNs (why, and the next source)

1. **The 96-byte body entry fields.** The array is located (`body_entries`, 62 entries × 96 B at a
   recorded pointer) and sized, but the field names are not decoded (G2-30 UNKNOWN 4). *Next
   source:* a layout pass validated by the parts module - a body entry whose first u32 were a shape
   pointer would have to resolve, for the 42 part bodies, to the 54 polytope objects in a stable
   order; that test has not been run.
2. **`hknpShapeMassProperties` (56 × 48 B, layout not decoded).** No mass, inertia tensor or centre
   of mass is read; the first blob is stored verbatim so a future layout can be tested against it.
   *Next source:* the Havok 2014 public header for `hknpShapeMassProperties`, then a containment
   test (centre of mass inside the shape's AABB; positive-definite inertia) across the 56.
3. **Which bodies the 11 constraints join.** Axis/pivot/limit layout is NAMED-ONLY for both
   `hkpLimitedHingeConstraintData` and `hkpRagdollConstraintData`; only class, offset and extent
   are known. *Next source:* the constraint body references, which sit in the packfile's fixup
   tables - the same mechanism already used for shapes, applied to these 11 objects.
4. **The 24 `raycast_body_id` values below 42.** They are not in the extern-mesh window; what they
   index is UNKNOWN (a per-part raycast body table that no decoder names).
5. **What references the two static compounds (7 + 7).** No pointer from a body entry to a
   compound is decoded, and the parts module has no compound id. *Next source:* the body-entry
   layout above, or the `CPfxPartIndexProperty` objects (98 in the donor, never decoded).
6. **The model ↔ physics frame relation, and the collision layer.** Not in this file: the frame
   link was left conditional by G3-01, and no collision-layer field is decoded here.
