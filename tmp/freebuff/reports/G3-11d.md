## 4. Per-row detail

### `T-CO1` — PARTIAL
- **"32 boxes (bone, centre, extents)"**: 32 is T-22's body count; the decoded box census is 131.
  Asset/element labels (bone, centre, extents) are right on all 131 records.
- **"`.physicsc` collision shapes"**: right — 54 hulls, 18 extern meshes, 36 geometries,
  22 compounds, 112 instances of which 98 carry a part index.
- **"donor's shapes, stations moved"**: donor shapes yes; the station move is in 3 of 4 sha
  groups (52/87 trees, `hknpPhysicsSystemData` changed) and absent from the 35 vanilla-identical
  trees and from B-10 → UNKNOWN-per-build, not a fact about "the build".

### `T-CO2` — MISMATCH
- the triple exists and equals T-22's recorded largest (left rear wheel), **but** the largest
  decoded box is the drivable chassis at 690.305 cm — 374.2 cm longer than the triple's longest
  side. The row's superlative holds only inside T-22's wreck set.
- `1 cm = 0.01 m` → 3.1615 × 0.6342 × 1.0988 m: correct arithmetic.
- containment recomputed from hull vertices: the 3.1615 m box fits hull `@21072` with margins
  `[0.5778, 0.9862, 0.5173]` m (sorted axes, axis-agnostic). The 6.90 m chassis box fits **no**
  hull — the donor's longest hull axis is 3.679 m. So the fit argument is valid for the box named,
  invalid for the largest box that exists.

### `T-CO3` — MATCHES
- recounted from the donor part table: **38/42** parts have a world collision shape; the 4 without
  are `sportsmuscle_wheel_r_l`, `wheel_r_r`, `wheel_f_l`, `wheel_f_r`, all with
  `world_collision_body_id = -1`. (`sportsmuscle_steeringwheel` is not a road wheel: id 27.)
- G3-10 agrees the wheels are sim-only (world -1, raycast ids 22/23/36/37).

### `T-P3` — PARTIAL
- "32 collision bodies (boxes)": 32 bodies, categories `{root 1, wheel/suspension 8, debris 18,
  turret 1, cockpit 1, door 2, jet fin 1}` = 32; box total still 131.
- "11 driveable boxes map onto 7 donor hull slots; 21 not driveable": reproduces T-22 exactly
  (`mapped_bodies 11`, `mapped_to_a_slot 7`, `unmapped 21`); 21 = 18 debris + 1 root + 1 turret +
  1 jet fin. The decoded file has two static compounds with **7 instances each** and
  **14 unbound** instances, which is where "7 slots" comes from; G3-10 marks the
  compound→body reference UNKNOWN, so the 11→7 **map** is not decoded.
- "11 T-8 parts have no slot": UNKNOWN here (the T-8 part table is not read by this card).

### `T-PA3` — PARTIAL
- donor part table: `body` 0, `door_f_l` 1, `engine` 7, the four road wheels `-1` — exact.
- "in the built graph": UNKNOWN — the built `.epe`'s own ids are not read here.

## 7. What this changes for G3-11's flag

- The collision rows are **not** uniformly stale: T-CO3 is exact, and T-P3/T-PA3 are exact in
  every number they quote from T-22 — their only weaknesses are the "boxes" noun and the parts of
  the claim that live outside the `.physicsc` (T-8 part list, built `.epe`).
- Two rows do carry a real error: **T-CO1/T-P3 call 32 "boxes"** when 32 is bodies and 131 boxes
  are decoded; **T-CO2's "largest box"** is the largest *wreck* box, while the drivable chassis box
  is 690.305 cm — so the "fits one donor hull at 1 cm" argument does not apply to the largest box.
- "stations moved" cannot be settled as a single fact: 52 of 87 build trees carry changed
  `hknpPhysicsSystemData`, 35 are byte-identical to vanilla, and B-10 changes hulls only.
