# G3-10c — constraint partner side, 96-byte body-entry fields, inertia

STATUS: **partial.** Three of the four questions are answered from the bytes; the inertia tensor is
not. The value that matters for translation (which body each constraint acts on, and the mass) is
decoded; the 24 packed bytes that would give the tensor are measured but not decoded, and are recorded
as UNKNOWN rather than fitted.

Done:
- **The cinfo record is dumped in full** (six words). It is `{constraintData* (fixup), bodyId,
  0x7FFFFFFF, 0, 0}` — 24 bytes. **No second body id exists** in it: no word other than the one body id
  is a body index, in the donor or in any of the 40 decodable fleet files. The partner side is
  therefore *not stored per-cinfo*; G3-10b's chassis hypothesis is neither confirmed nor refuted.
- **Constraint type at +0xC0**, decoded and fleet-wide: **18** `hkpLimitedHingeConstraintData`,
  **19** `hkpRagdollConstraintData`, **12** `hkpHingeConstraintData` (4 in the fleet, none in the
  donor).
- **Body-entry fields newly named:** `+0x0C` = the body's own id for the 38 part bodies
  (`0x7FFFFFFF` for the other 24, including 22/23/36/37); `+0x10` = `(id << 16) | flag` with flag
  `0xFF`, or `0x01` for bodies 11, 15, 27; `+0x3C` = 1.0; `+0x4C` = quaternion w (`0x3F7FFFFF`, and
  `0x3F800000` in the last two entries).
- **The mass↔hull link G3-10b could not find:** `polytope + 0x20` → `hkRefCountedProperties` →
  `+0x10` → the `hknpShapeMassProperties` block. **54 of 54** donor polytopes resolve (G3-10b: 0).

Not done: **the inertia tensor.** The 24 bytes at mass+0x10..+0x27 are not IEEE floats (as f32 they
are sub-1e-6 noise). Word `+0x1C` rises with mass (Spearman 0.95 over the 54 linked hulls), but
`word / 2^31 / mass` is **not** constant (relative spread 2.65), so it is neither a shifted-exponent
mass nor a decoded tensor. The six words are recorded verbatim for the next layout pass.

## 1. The constraint cinfo, all six words (decoded)

Donor `Builds/test-091/…/sportsmuscle.physicsc`, `hknpPhysicsSystemData` +0x50 → array of 11 records,
24 bytes each. Every record on disk:

```
0x4898 ... 00000003 ...                                        body 3
0x48B0 ... 00000004 ...                                        body 4

0x4970 ... 00000023 ...                                        body 35
```

**Decoded negative:** word 3 is `0x7FFFFFFF` in all 11, words 4 and 5 are zero — the same sentinel the
body table uses for bodies without a part id. So the second side is not in this record, and no
"second body id" field was invented for it.

**Type tag, decoded:** +0xC0 = 18/19 in the donor, 12 for the four `hkpHingeConstraintData` elsewhere.

**Derived hint (not a decode):** 16 of 24 decoded atom pivots fall inside the first polytope's bounds
(body 0's hull), which is consistent with a chassis partner but does not prove it.

## 2. Body entries (96 B) — the words this pass names

| word | what | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| +0x0C | body id | = entry index for the 38 part bodies; `0x7FFFFFFF` for the other 24 (22/23/36/37 included) |
| +0x10 | `(id << 16) | flag` | flag `0xFF`, or `0x01` at bodies 11, 15, 27; `0x000000FF` for the rest |
| +0x3C | translation w | `0x3F800000` in every entry |
| +0x4C | quaternion w | `0x3F7FFFFF` (0.99999994), `0x3F800000` in the last two |

Fleet oracle: the id rule holds in all 40 decoded files (`body_id_rule_all`), and the flag rule holds
once the `0x01` exceptions are allowed (`body_tag10_rule_all`). The remaining words keep G3-10b's
measured domains and stay UNKNOWN.

The 24 bytes are not floats. Word +0x1C rises with mass (Spearman **0.95**, oracle

The tensor itself stays UNKNOWN.

1. **The constraints' partner body.** Not in the cinfo (decoded negative above). Where it would be:
   the body table's `+0x20` pointer graph, or the `.ee` entity's constraint list. The pivot-containment
   hint is recorded as `derived`, not used.
2. **Inertia in `hknpShapeMassProperties`.** The 24 bytes are a packed encoding; only a rank relation
   to mass is measured, no tensor. Test that would settle it: decode candidate formats (3-byte
   mantissa + exponent, 16-bit fixed, packed unit vectors) and require each axis to match
   `m·(a²+b²)/12` from the linked hull within the hull's own tolerance.
3. **The `+0x01` flag at bodies 11/15/27.** The field is decoded as `(id<<16)|flag`; what the flag
   means (0x01 vs 0xFF) is not in the bytes read.
4. **Remaining body-entry words.** Their fleet domains are measured (G3-10b §3); no names.
