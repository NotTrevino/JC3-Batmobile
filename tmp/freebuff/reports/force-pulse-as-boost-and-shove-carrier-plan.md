# `CForcePulse` as the carrier for three BmAK impulse behaviours — decode, verdicts, build gaps

**Card target.** BmAK has three impulse-like behaviours the build wants: the **boost kick**
(a one-shot lateral/forward punch), the **road-assist shove** (a short, targeted push that keeps the
car pointing down the road) and the **2,000,000 kill-flip torque** (a hard roll applied to a car the
Batmobile hits). This report decodes `CForcePulse` from the two named files, then asks of each
behaviour: can a pulse carry it, with which field values, fired how — and what must the build add
where it cannot.

**Files read.** `Research/JC3Grab/FORCEPULSE.json` (28,992 lines, 307 pulses), `Research/JC3Grab/FORCEPULSE.md`
(the G2-17 record), `Research/JC3Grab/EMP.md` / `EMP.json` / `EMP_DISABLE.json`,
`Research/JC3Grab/WEAPONS.md`, `Research/JC3Grab/ENTITIES.md`, `Research/BatmobileGrab/MODES.md`,
`Research/BatmobileGrab/DAMAGE_RULES.md`, `Research/specs/G2-35.md`, `reports/stuck-flip-scrape.md`,
`tmp/freebuff/reports/immobiliser-and-emp-trigger.md`, `Tools/scripts/bm_entity.py`.
Plus one derivation pass over `FORCEPULSE.json` run for this report (cross-tab of every pulse's
hashed members against its tune; output quoted in §2.3).

**`reports/G2-17.md` does not exist.** `ls reports/` holds only `emp-effects-scrape-leads.md` and
`stuck-flip-scrape.md`. The G2-17 deliverable on disk is `Research/JC3Grab/FORCEPULSE.md` (+ `.json`),
generated by `Tools/scripts/jc3forcepulse.py`; it is the file this report decodes. Recorded as a
missing input, not worked around by guesswork.

**Labels.** **decoded** = a value read from a named member in a shipped file. **derived** = a stated
rule over decoded values (the rule is named). **UNKNOWN** = no member, field or body found, with the
file that would settle it. Nothing below is filled in from engine knowledge, and no field is given a
name the corpus does not have.

## 1. What `CForcePulse` decodes to

A pulse is an entity object (parented under the vehicle root, or under a `CRigidObject` / weapon
component). 307 of them in 232 vehicle `.ee` over 79 chassis. It has **31 members**: 5 named
(`SettingsFile`, `_object_id`, `name`, `name_hash`, `world`) and 26 Jenkins-hashed `id:*` keys whose
names are in no shipped name table (UNKNOWN — `FORCEPULSE.md` §1). Its `SettingsFile` resolves to an
embedded `<tune>.ftunec` of class `ForcePulseSettings`, which decodes to **exactly eight numbers**,
all damage and radius.

### 1.1 The eight tune numbers — the only quantities with names

| member (decoded) | type | meaning carried by the name | fleet range | evidence |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| `PlayerDamage` | uint32 | damage to the player's side | 0 … 3000 | decoded |
| `PlayerDamageRadius` | float, **metres** | outer radius | 1.5 … 50 | decoded |
| `PlayerDamageRadiusPercentageMax` | float 0–1 | fraction of the radius that keeps full damage | 0 … 1 | decoded name; arithmetic **derived** |
| `PlayerDamageRadiusMin` | float | inner full-damage bound | 0 … 1 | decoded name; arithmetic **derived** |
| `AIDamage` / `AIDamageRadius` / `AIDamageRadiusPercentageMax` / `AIDamageRadiusMin` | same | the AI-side twin | 0/5 … 1000/50 | decoded |

The unit is metres, not engine units: the JC3 corpus stores every length in metres (EMP pulse at
`(0, 2.0356, 0.6992)`, radius 25 m for a truck flatbed; `gen_explosion_5m_nodamage` radius 5). Evidence:
decoded positions/radii in `FORCEPULSE.json`, cross-checked against `EMP.md` §2.

**Falloff rule (derived).** Across the 23 tunes the two bounds move together with the radius:
`0/0` on the stock vehicle blasts (full damage only at the centre, falling off immediately),
`0.1/0.25` on `barrel_explosion` and `bike_explosion_medium`, `0.25/0.25` on the fuel tank and
`w704_m488`, `1.0/1.0` on the EMP `truck_emp` and `w613_bomb`. The reading that fits every tune is:
full damage out to `max(RadiusPercentageMax·R, RadiusMin·R)`, then a falloff to zero at `R`. **The
shape of the falloff (linear, quadratic, inverse-square) is UNKNOWN** — see §6.

### 1.2 Space

The pulse carries a `world` matrix (a 4×3 transform, translation in the last column, metres). 292 of
307 sit at the entity origin; the exceptions are the EMP device `(0, 2.0356, 0.6992)`, two cargo
pulses at `(0, 0.2613, 5.5002)`, a barrel at `(-9.616, -2.3759, -4.2695)` and ten at
`(0.0129, 0.5109, 0)`. Six matrices are non-identity and carry rotation + translation only (columns
are unit length — **derived** from the printed matrices).

So the blast centre is expressed **in the parent entity's space** and applied **in world space at
that point**: the parent transform places it, and the pulse is radial about that point. Evidence:
decoded `world` member; the placement of the EMP pulse over the flatbed device matches the truck's
own part positions (`EMP.md` §2). **Not decoded:** whether the radius is measured in world axes or
in the parent's axes — the matrices are rotation-only, so at unit scale the two agree, and no pulse
in the fleet carries a scale that would distinguish them (derived).

### 1.3 Timing

**There is no time member anywhere.** The 8 tune numbers contain no duration, delay, tick, interval
or lifetime; the 31 pulse members contain none either (`FORCEPULSE.md` §1, and the 31-member census
in `FORCEPULSE.json`). A pulse is therefore a **one-shot event**: one blast at one instant, no
sustain. That is the single most important structural fact for this card — all three BmAK behaviours
are *duration* behaviours (0.15–0.25 s for the kick, 0.333 s for the shove, 1.5 s for the kill-flip).

### 1.4 What the pulse does *not* have

Searched the full 31-member set of all 307 pulses and the 8 members of all 23 tunes:

| BmAK needs | member in `CForcePulse` / `ForcePulseSettings` |
|---|---|
| force / impulse magnitude | **none** |
| torque / angular magnitude | **none** |
| direction vector | **none** (direction is radial from the world transform) |
| duration / ramp | **none** |
| target class / filter | **none** |
| mass or velocity factor | **none** |

`FORCEPULSE.md` §1 lists the 26 hashed members with their value ranges and states plainly that their
names are not in any table. The only named quantity that behaves like an effect size is
`PlayerDamage`/`AIDamage`; everything else in the object is nameless.

### 1.5 The one inference the corpus already relies on, and its status

`MODLOG.md` (G2-17/G2-16 entries) and `FORCEPULSE.md` §3 describe `gen_explosion_5m_nodamage`
(0 damage / 5 m) as a **"push only"** tune, and `T-WP8` ships it as the sonar shockwave. That is an
**inference, not a decode**: no member of the pulse or its tune says "push", and the eight named
numbers are damage and radius only. It is the cheapest probe on this card (§6, probe **P-1**) and
every verdict below that depends on it is marked accordingly.

## 2. The three behaviours — field values from BmAK, converted

### 2.1 Boost kick

The kick has two decoded carriers in BmAK, and they are different shapes (both quoted from
`Research/BatmobileGrab/MODES.md`, cross-checked against `DAMAGE_RULES.md` and
`reports/stuck-flip-scrape.md` §2.4):

| member | offset | value | unit | evidence |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| `BattleDodgeImpulse` | 0x1914 | **150000** | engine **impulse** | decoded (3 of 4 packages; Joker has no tag → class default, UNKNOWN) |
| `PMDodgeForce` | 0x1fe0 | **10000** | engine **force** | decoded |
| `PMDodgeTorque` | 0x1fdc | **6000** | engine **torque** | decoded |
| `PMDodgeDuration` | 0x1fe4 | **0.15** | s | decoded |
| `StrafeCooldown` | 0x11e4 | 0.75 | s | decoded, CDO only |
| `KickStrafe(Force, Torque, Duration)` triple | — | **1200 / 2500 / 0.25** | engine force / engine torque / s | **derived** — the literals inside `VehicleStrafe` (export 10581), whose body is the only place these three numbers appear; operand order is not proven |

For this card the boost kick is taken as the **impulse form**, because that is the one BmAK
serialises as an impulse: `BattleDodgeImpulse` **150000 engine impulse units**, fired in **0.15 s**;
the `KickStrafe` triple (1200/2500/0.25) is the fallback derivation if the impulse path proves
unreachable.

**Unit conversion.** BmAK lengths are cm and its angles are degrees, but its force/torque/impulse
numbers are **UE3 engine units, for which the corpus explicitly refuses a conversion**
(`MODLOG.md` G1-21: "engine (torque/force/impulse/power magnitudes — UE3 documents no conversion, so
shipped numbers are carried as-is)"). This report keeps that rule and adds only what arithmetic
supports, clearly marked:

| conversion | result | status |
|---|---|---|
| 0.15 s, 0.25 s, 0.333 s, 1.5 s | unchanged (JC3 stores seconds) | decoded/derived as above |
| 45° | **0.785398 rad** | derived (π/180) |
| impulse 150000 → N·s | **1500 N·s** *if* the UE3 impulse unit is kg·cm/s (1 unit = 0.01 N·s) | **derived, unverified**; on an assumed 2,000 kg car → 0.75 m/s |
| torque 30000 / 2500 / 6000 / 2,000,000 → N·m | **not converted**; the same cm/kg identity would give 1e-4 N·m per unit and make 30000 equal 3 N·m, which is physically absurd for a car | **UNKNOWN**: the engine-unit → N·m factor is not in the corpus; carried as engine units |

So the only *usable* converted numbers are the durations (seconds), the angle (radians) and, as a
cross-check with a stated assumption, the impulse in N·s. Everything torque-side stays in engine
units and must be matched **by ratio and by observed effect**, not by arithmetic.

### 2.3 Kill-flip torque

| member | offset | CDO | V2 / MK2 / Funhouse | Joker | unit | evidence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| `KillFlipTorque` | 0x1c18 | **-2000000** | -200 | -400 | engine torque | decoded (`MODES.md:164`, CDO re-walked at 7192) |

### 2.4 Pulse-side values that would have to carry the three behaviours

Nothing in the pulse names a force. The only effect-size member is `PlayerDamage` (and `AIDamage`).
The nameless floats were cross-tabbed against the tune for this report (307 pulses, grouped by
`SettingsFile`):

| hashed member | distinct values over 307 | behaviour of the value |
|---|---:|---|
| `id:24872388` | 6 | 100 on all 115 car blasts, **600** on the fuel tank, **400** on the barrel, **25** on the three `+_impact` tunes, **1000** on `w704_m488` |
| `id:9865E7F2` | 5 | 100 / 40 / 50 / 25 / 250 — same shape, different scale |
| `id:060AAA81` | 3 | 20 (286), 5 (11 = fuel tank), 60 (10 = barrel) |
| `id:530046B3` | 9 | 10 cars, 30 fuel tank, 12 barrel, 0 on the impact tunes, **15 vs 20 inside the same `boat_explosion_large` tune** |
| `id:F39EC6EA` | 3 | 0.1 (287), 0.8 (fuel tank), 0.75 (barrel) — a 0–1 quantity |
| `id:A1B0DAF9` | 4 | 2 / 3 / −1 / 0 |
| `id:4912FC04`, `id:AE6F7EFF`, `id:CF4F7880`, `id:2849C306`, `id:5AF29116`, `id:831DAB81`, `id:34303F77` | 2 each | per-instance flags; the EMP's unique triple is `0 / 1 / 1` |

**What this proves and what it does not.** It proves the tuned quantities move with the blast
(five of them are tune-determined, so they *are* blast parameters, not build metadata), and it proves
`id:530046B3` is at least partly per-instance (15 vs 20 within one tune). It does **not** name any of
them, and no value in this table is a magnitude in any unit the corpus documents. **No field in the
pulse can be written as "torque = X" or "impulse = X" today.** That is the wall, and it is why the
verdicts below are about *shape*, not numbers.

## 3. Verdicts — can a pulse carry each behaviour?

### 3.1 Boost kick — **NO** (a pulse cannot push its own carrier)

**Derived.** The pulse is a blast **radial about its own world transform**; a body whose centre sits
at that transform receives a force along `(bodyPos − pulsePos)`, i.e. a zero-length vector. 292 of
307 pulses sit exactly at the entity origin, so mounting one on the car to kick the car gives
**zero** net impulse on the car by construction, and an off-origin mount gives a push that rotates
the car rather than accelerating it along its heading.

**Decoded support.** No impulse, force or direction member exists (§1.4); the pulse's only named
effect is damage. Whether the blast applies a non-damage push at all is the P-1 inference (§1.5).

**Consequence.** The kick needs a **self-impulse along the car's own axes** — the exact opposite of
what a radial blast does. The pulse is the wrong primitive for this behaviour, regardless of tuning.

### 3.2 Road-assist shove — **PARTIAL** (direction and radius yes; magnitude, duration and target class no)

The shove acts on **another** body, and there the radial geometry is right: BmAK shoves the other car
away from the Batmobile and adds torque to keep it pointing down the road; a pulse centred on (or
just ahead of) the Batmobile pushes every body in radius **away from the Batmobile**. That is the
shove's direction without any direction field.

| requirement | pulse provides | status |
|---|---|---|
| push direction = away from the car | radial falloff about the pulse transform, placed on the car | decoded transform + derived geometry |
| reach | `PlayerDamageRadius` in metres (fleet 1.5–50) | decoded |
| strength profile across the reach | `*RadiusPercentageMax` / `*RadiusMin`; set 1.0/1.0 for a flat push | decoded members, derived reading |
| no damage to the target | `gen_explosion_5m_nodamage` = 0/5 | decoded tune; "0 damage still pushes" **inferred** (P-1) |
| magnitude (30000 engine torque, factor 90000) | **no member** | UNKNOWN |
| 0.333 s duration | **no member** — one shot | decoded absence |
| target class / enemy-vs-scenery gating | **no member** — everything in radius | decoded absence |
| angular assist (keep the target pointing down the road) | **no member** — the push is through the target's centre, so it imparts translation, not the assist torque | derived |

**Pulse values for the shove (this is the whole field set the pulse can take):**
`PlayerDamage` 0, `AIDamage` 0, `PlayerDamageRadius` = **reach**, `AIDamageRadius` = same,
`PercentageMax` = `RadiusMin` = **1.0** (flat through the radius, because BmAK's shove is flat for
its duration), `world` translation on the car's nose at `(0, 0, +half-length)`.
Reach is **derived** from the one BmAK time the corpus does hold — the assist looks ahead
`LookAheadTime` 1.0 s (G2-35, class default) — so `reach ≈ v · 1.0 s`: 10 m at 36 km/h, 28 m at
100 km/h. The fleet has both 5 m and 10 m no/low-damage tunes, so 5–25 m is inside shipped range.

### 3.3 Kill-flip torque (2,000,000 over 1.5 s) — **NO**

A torque needs either an angular member (none exists) or a force applied **off** the target's centre
of mass (no force member exists). The pulse has neither; it pushes through the body centre radially,
which by construction produces no torque about the body's own centre. Add the missing duration
(1.5 s vs one shot) and the missing target selection (the kill-flip applies to the car the Batmobile
*hits*, not to everything in radius), and the pulse fails all three of the behaviour's defining
properties.

**Consequence.** The kill-flip must be a native torque writer (`AddTorqueOverTime`-shaped). The
pulse's only possible contribution is the *pairing*: a pulse on the impact event could be the marker
that a native hook reads to start the 1.5 s torque. That is a design note, not a decoded route.

## 4. How a pulse is triggered from a vehicle or weapon entity

### 4.1 The decoded trigger: the owner pointer

`id:B5AE7986` is set **on the owner** and holds the pulse's `_object_id`. It is the only member that
links a pulse to anything (293 of 307 pulses; 1 unrelated reference exists, §4.3).

| owner class | pulses | meaning carried by the pairing | evidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `CCar` | 126 | the vehicle's **own destruction blast** — every one uses that vehicle type's explosion tune | decoded |
| `CBoat` 35, `CHelicopter` 23, `CAirPlane` 18, `CTrain` 12 | 88 | same, per class | decoded |
| `CBulletWeaponBase` 29, `CMissileWeaponBase` 25, `CRPGFireLeachWeapon` 6 | 60 | **the weapon's own blast**, one per wired weapon, using that weapon's `jc_explosions/weapons/<id>.ftune` | decoded |
| `(none)` | 14 | barrel cargo ×10, loose cargo ×2, EMP ×1, mm340 rule ×1 | decoded absence |

**What the pointer proves and what it does not.** It proves *who carries* which blast, exactly, and
that the pairing is class-consistent (a vehicle carries its own explosion; a weapon carries its own
impact blast). It does **not** name the event that reads it: the firing moment of the vehicle blast
and the exact weapon instant (fire vs impact) are **UNKNOWN** — code, not data (`FORCEPULSE.md` §4.2,
Q-1).

**Therefore, the two decoded wiring routes are:**

1. **Vehicle entity:** set `id:B5AE7986` on the `CCar` root to the new pulse's `_object_id`. What the
   engine does with it is the vehicle's destruction blast. Using it for a *non-destruction* event is
   not supported by any file — the pointer is the crash/explosion blast by pairing.
2. **Weapon entity:** set `id:B5AE7986` on a `CBulletWeaponBase` / `CMissileWeaponBase` component to
   the pulse's `_object_id`, and give the weapon the matching blast tune. This is the **only decoded
   route in which a player action produces a pulse**: fire the weapon → its blast tune is the pulse.
   It is also the route the build already uses for the missile (`w618`), and the reason a cloned
   `w618` + a pulse is the standard recipe in `T-WP4` / `T-WP5`.

### 4.2 The three non-owner routes on record

| route | what the files show | status |
|---|---|---|
| use-item (EMP) | `trigger.use_item` + `entry.use_item` → state `S_STUNT_USE_ITEM_VEHICLE`, prompt `ui_activate_emp`, **no pointer to the pulse** | inference, **UNKNOWN** (Q-EMP-2) |
| graph script | `v4603_plane_urga_cargotransport_mm340.ee` `CGraphScriptGameObject` → `id:65424AC6` → the `BOMB_JEEP` pulse | decoded pointer, bytecode **UNKNOWN** |
| shape / event trigger | `CShapeTrigger` 32, `CEventTrigger` 18 objects in the fleet; **no member carries damage or impulse**, and none references a pulse | decoded absence (G2-35 §2) |

### 4.3 The build's current state

`Tools/scripts/bm_entity.py` already adds two pulse objects to the built entity —
`CForcePulse_EMP` (T-WP5, the truck's EMP pulse + `truck_emp.ftune` + the flag triple) and
`CForcePulse_Sonar` (T-WP8, the 0-damage 5 m tune) — and zeroes `id:D3DDD766` on the EMP copy.
A search of that file for `B5AE7986` / `owner` returns **0 matches**: the build appends the pulses and
never writes the owner pointer. So today the built pulses are carried but **wired to nothing**, which
matches the fleet's 14 unowned pulses and is exactly the gap this card has to close.

## 5. What the build must add

| # | behaviour | what the pulse gives | what the build must add | where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | boost kick | nothing usable (self-radial blast = 0) | a **self-impulse writer**: forward/lateral impulse along the car's axes for 0.15–0.25 s. Closest decoded JC3 carrier is `arcade.nitro_boost.push_force` **15000** (continuous, `G2-35` §2) or `arcade.turbo_jump.*` (fixed-axis punch); neither is event-shaped, so the event half is native | existing handling module rows + a native hook card (R17); a pulse may be *added* as the visual/sound marker only |
| 2 | road-assist shove | direction (radially away from the car), reach (radius), flat strength (1.0/1.0), 0 damage | (a) the **owner pointer** `id:B5AE7986` so the pulse is carried by a weapon component or the root; (b) a **trigger** that fires it on contact (weapon-impact route is decoded; shape-trigger route is not); (c) the **duration** (0.333 s) and the **assist torque** — no member exists, so a native writer; (d) the target-class gate | `bm_entity.py` for (a); a weapon clone for (b); native card for (c)+(d) |
| 3 | kill-flip (−2,000,000 / 1.5 s) | nothing (no torque, no duration, no target) | a **targeted torque-over-time writer** on the impacted car: 2,000,000 engine units for 1.5 s (or the shipped −200/−400 for 0.5/0.75 s). BmAK's own implementation is native (`FlipVehicle` uses `AddTorqueOverTime` + `AddKeepyUpForce`) | native card only; a pulse can mark the hit (sound/FX) but cannot carry the torque |

**One structural point the build inherits.** All three additions are *duration* writers, and a
`CForcePulse` has no duration member (§1.3). Even if a nameless float turned out to be an impulse,
the pulse would still be a one-frame event. So the honest build shape is: **native writers for the
three behaviours, pulses for their blast/impact signatures** — not "a pulse tuned three ways".

## 7. Pilot-card sentences (one per behaviour, with the pass band)

**B-1 · boost kick.**
*"At a standstill, on the kick input, the car gains lateral velocity and nothing else."*
**Pass band:** lateral Δv **0.6–1.5 m/s** reached within **0.25 s** of the input (the impulse-form
number is 150000 engine units ≈ 0.75 m/s on an assumed 2,000 kg car), heading change **≤ 8°**,
forward speed change **≤ 10 %**, hull health unchanged, and the same kick from a second input within
0.75 s (the `StrafeCooldown`). **Fail:** no lateral motion (no writer), motion above 2 m/s (writer
