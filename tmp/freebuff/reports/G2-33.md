## Findings worth carrying forward

- **Two light lists, not one.** The module `VehicleDynamicLights` has 3,217 entries over 223
  vehicles, 25 members each, and is the only side that binds a light to a part. The `.epe` graph
  has 3,972 named `SVehicleLight` objects with 404 preset names but no part, no index and no
  transform, and its `preset_id` is a string where the module's is a uint32 hash. Nothing joins
  the two; which one the renderer reads is UNKNOWN.
- **Presets do not carry the numbers.** 17 shipped entries use `preset_id = 0` and 34 presets
  appear with different value tuples on different vehicles, so an entry is described by its own
  members.
- **`has_lights` is a flag, not a gate**: 3 entries miss their part index, 27 name a different
  part, 6 sit on a `has_lights = 0` part, and 13 lit parts carry no entry.
- **`CarLight` is uniform** (11,367 blocks: stride 12 B, uint16 indices, exactly 6 material slots)
  but its parameter bytes are still undecoded (§102d) — copy, never author.
- **The wheel part cannot host the light as-is**: `has_lights = 0` and `CarPaintMM`-only meshes,
  which is why the recipe adds a part with a `CarLight` block rather than editing the wheel.
