How the search reads the banks (decoded): each bank is its own `AkBank` export — the payload is
the export's `[offset, offset+size)` range of the package image, decompressed block by block, so
only the blocks a payload occupies are inflated. The chunk chain is located at `BKHD` and walked
by `4-char magic + u32 size` to the payload end. Events are HIRC objects of type `0x04`, id =
first `u32` of the object body (`u8 type + u32 size` headers). Four oracles guard the statement:
`O-1` every payload's SHA-1 equals `AUDIO_BANKS.json`'s recorded hash for that export (117/117);
`O-2` every HIRC walk tiles its chunk and its Event count equals the recorded `events_in_bank`;
`O-3` the review's name rule `fnv1(lower(name))` reproduces all 12 declared pairs, so a miss is a
fact about the bank, not about the lookup; `O-4` the raw u32 search and the HIRC parse agree on
every payload (`0x6BB2C66E` is found by neither). `O-2` also records that 73 of the 117 payloads
are `DLG_*` exports whose `BKHD` bank sits at a non-zero offset with a `BKPK` blob after it (24
distinct offsets); those blobs are raw-searched by `O-4` but not parsed as containers.
