## Answer in one paragraph

No vehicle in the grab launches its driver upward, and the data cannot: all **4,832**
`CInteractionGraphExit` objects (232 graphs, 61 names, 26 in the donor) draw their members from a
fixed 9-key set with **0** velocity/impulse/launch/force members; the only graph class with a
moving-sounding name is the velocity *condition* (plus the car's own `CForcePulse`), and the only
upward mechanism in the modules is `arcade.turbo_jump` (the **car** jumps; no vertical-speed
member). An exit's job is to pick a state, gated by the parent's speed: the donor splits at **5**,
so `exit.driver.moving.parachute` → `S_EXIT_VEHICLE_PARA` is simply *the fast exit* (on 221/232
vehicles). BmAK's eject is a special-move set (`EjectUpMove`, `EjectForwardMove`, `…VerticalMove`,
`EjectToSpotMove`) plus `EjectUpDist` and a native `ModifyEjectUpLoc`; **the mapping is exit →
`exit.driver.moving.parachute`, launch → UNKNOWN.**
