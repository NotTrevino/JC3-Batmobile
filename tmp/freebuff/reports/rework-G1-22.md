## The one-line diagnosis

The population work was right; the **relevance judgement** was not. Twenty `Function` exports were
filed as irrelevant on the strength of a sentence saying their behaviour was already decoded by
G1-08/G1-14 — none of those cards contains them — and the component section called 14–62 B
payloads "header-only" when seven of them carry a float. On top of that the note had two typed
numbers that disagreed with its own data (`768 ... Package stubs`, `5 + 17`), and `--check` never
read the note.

## Issue-by-issue

### 1. `Function` = irrelevant on a false provenance claim — CONFIRMED (R4/R5, blocking)

The review's evidence reproduces exactly.

```
$ for n in FireWeapon StartFiring FireAtTarget PlayHitReaction; do ... grep -c ...; done

StartFiring        WEAPONS:0 DAMAGE:0
FireAtTarget       WEAPONS:0 DAMAGE:0
PlayHitReaction    WEAPONS:0 DAMAGE:0

$ grep -n "DamagedBy" Research/BatmobileGrab/DAMAGE_RULES.md
108:   the `Cloudburst` flag `bDamagedByCloudburstTank`, the beacon type `RCommandBeaconBase`;
$ grep -n "BeginState" Research/BatmobileGrab/ASSETS.md | head -1
410:- **Function** (18): `BeginState`, `CheckForDeadThugs`, `DamagedBy`, ...
```

`ASSETS.md:410` is a name listing, not a decode. The payloads are live bytecode:

```
FireAtTarget   idx 59   793B nz=553 head=00 00 31 00 00 00 35 fe ff ff ...
PlayHitReaction idx 102 688B nz=532 head=00 00 63 00 00 00 1a fe ff ff ...
FireWeapon     idx 30   548B nz=363 head=00 00 15 00 00 00 5a fd ff ff ...
DamagedBy      idx 93   218B nz=150 head=00 00 5a 00 00 00 6e fe ff ff ...
```

20 rows, 5,562 B, 45–793 B each, 17–553 non-zero bytes. **Fix:** `Function` is now classified
**relevant** (`kind: function`); `second_pass` records `outcome: bytecode` with the non-zero byte
count; the "already decoded" sentence is deleted. I did **not** attempt to decode UE3 bytecode —
the record says the literals' meaning is UNKNOWN, which is the honest half of the review's
alternative. New checks: "Function exports are relevant, not irrelevant" and "every Function row
records undecoded bytecode".
