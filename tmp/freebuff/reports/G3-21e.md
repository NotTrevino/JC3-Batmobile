## Method (as stated in the document)

* **Cutoff**: a report is in the delta when its mtime is later than the baseline's — both printed to
  the second.
* **Attachment**: the card family in the file name must be a card the baseline lists under R-n
  (core or supporting tables). `review-`/`rework-`/`audit-`/`chase-`/`polish-`/`reverify-` prefixes
  are stripped, the longest card name wins, and then the family (`g3-10`, `g3-02`, …) is tried; if a
  family maps to several items or to none, the report stays unattached. Every row prints how it was
  attached (`card \`G3-10b\`` or `card family \`g3-10\``).
* **Status**: the report's own `STATUS:` line, quoted. Only the words `done` and `partial` count as
  evidence; `blocked` is C-2's no-evidence case; any other word (`fixed`, `needs rework`, …) is
  quoted and treated as no evidence rather than upgraded.

## UNKNOWNs

- **Whether a "closure" is a real closure.** The tool states a mechanical rule (content-word
  coverage ≥ 0.5 in the body, and the same unknown not restated in the report's own list) and prints
  every coverage. It does not read the claims and does not judge them: a report could meet the words
  without resolving the question. Each closure is therefore a *pointer to the report to read*, with
  the numbers that produced it, not a verdict.
- **Which status word a review's `STATUS:` uses.** 37 of the 117 delta reports use a word outside
  INSTRUCTIONS §4's `done | partial | blocked` (`needs rework`, `fixed`, …). They are quoted verbatim
  and **not** counted as evidence; if the operator wants `needs rework` to subtract from an item,
  that rule is not in the baseline and was not invented here.
- **Reports with no card family.** 47 delta reports (audio, ledger, world/translation, build cards)
  attach to no R item. The document lists them with their STATUS and reason instead of guessing.
- **The R verdicts themselves.** Not re-derived; the baseline's PARTIAL×6 stands as quoted. This card
  answers "what changed in the reports", not "what should the verdict be now".
