# T-WLD11 - the three stunt areas, the deform-point table, and where they must move

Offline study of the donor `StuntAreas` + `DeformPoints` against the Batmobile body that
ships in `Builds/bm-RC2`. No archive is opened, no game is launched, no build file is
touched.

**Answer in one line:** the three areas are authored blankets 0.039-0.158 m above the
Serpente's skin and are still stored at Serpente coordinates in the Batmobile entity. On
the RC2 body they sit **0.19-0.31 m below the surface they mean to cover** (stored frame)
or **0.32-0.49 m above it** (bbox-scaled frame). A spine-fitted coordinate set is derived
below that puts **22/22 vertices** on a standing face inside the donor band, but a
two-column blanket still chords the Batmobile's roof vents and hood step, so the honest
target is a **three-column ribbon**, which needs deform indices **above 134** (none exist)

edits the points and lets the copy follow. Which field the runtime trusts is **UNKNOWN**
(§8).

## 4. Proposed coordinates - the spine-fit rule (P-1)

### 4.1 The rule

For each of the 22 bound vertices:

1. **Row z**: keep the donor row order and use T-WLD11B's row map (`target_rows` in
   `t_wld11b_surfaces.json`): each donor row's fraction of its **host band** applied to
   the **target patch's** standing z span.
   - hood host band [-2.3143, -0.7295] -> target patch [-2.9872, -1.1675]:
     -0.9229 / -1.7234 / -2.2526 -> **-1.390 / -2.309 / -2.916**;
   - roof host band [-1.0212, 2.5015] -> target patch standing span:
     -0.9229 / -0.0758 / +0.8805 / +2.1333 / +2.4139 ->
     **-1.039 / +0.057 / +1.294 / +2.914 / +3.097**.
2. **Row half-width**: the standing x-run that contains x = 0 at that z (if none, the
   widest run within |x| < 0.75), capped at **0.30 m** and inset 0.03 m from its edges.
   That yields 0.10 m (cowl), 0.30 m (hood, roof plates) and 0.14-0.16 m (roof spine rows).
3. **y**: top standing surface at that (x, z), **plus the vertex's own decoded donor
   clearance** (`T_WLD11_AREAS.md` §3: 0.1056, 0.1056, 0.1578, 0.1219, 0.1219, 0.1569 for
   the hood; 0.1056, 0.0885, 0.0999, 0.0733, 0.0389, 0.0388, 0.0716, 0.0903, 0.0742,
   0.1056 for the roof). The clearance is the donor's own authored air gap and is kept
   so the feel does not change.
4. **Rear exception (measured, not chosen):** at the mapped rear rows z +2.914 and
   +3.097 the RC2 body has **no standing run wider than 0.06 m** (rear deck slope
   up-cos 0.2-0.58; tail standing surface 2 cm wide). The rows are therefore moved
   forward, in 0.02 m steps, to the last rows that do have a standing run at least
   0.15 m half-wide: **z +2.400 and +2.583**. Moved distance: **0.514 m and 0.514 m**.
   That is the one discretionary step in P-1, and it shortens `main_area` by 0.51 m
   (plan length 4.14 m -> 3.62 m, -12.5%).

## 7. What is still UNKNOWN

- **Runtime precedence** between `StuntAreas.vertices3D_local` and the bound
  `DeformPoints.start_transform`. Nothing in the entity, the geom sidecar or the modules
  states which one the engine trusts. The 0.0 m invariant means a one-sided edit creates
  a self-contradicting file.
- **Whether the areas are validated at runtime** against the deform list (a mismatch is
  exactly what P-1 would create if only one side is written).
- **Whether `DeformPoints` also feeds deformation/skinning.** If it does, moving a point
  moves geometry, not just a standing plane - the 22 indices include panel points
  (`hood`, `trunk`, `door_f_l_hinge_a`, ...), though only the 22 bound ones would move.
- **Whether the `.vmodc` vertex/triangle arrays may grow.** The decoded entity arrays are
  48 floats / 42 indices (16 verts / 14 tris); the module's own schema was not decoded.
- **Whether the RC2 add-on parts** (turret, missile pods, aero flaps) sit above the
  hood/roof band. `body_lod1.rbm` is the body only; their transforms were not part of
  this task.
- **Which body the player stands on**: the appearance source (32,124 tris) or the RC2
  body (44,608 tris). P-1 is measured on RC2 because that is what ships. The appearance
  mesh cannot answer at two rows (its ray at z +0.057 passes into the cabin, 1.2596 m;
  at z +2.400 it hits the floor pan, 0.1806 m), so its geometry is not watertight there;
  where both answer they agree (z -1.039: 2.0045 vs 2.0008; z +1.294: 2.1127 both).
