# Lock hold and multi-target salvo: every place JC3 could carry them

**Answer: neither is in JC3's data.** A lock *hold time* and a *target count* have no member in the Fire Leech's AutoAim tune, in any of the 15 AutoAim tunes, in any of the 103 weapon tunes, in any of the 59 `jc_weapons` entity graphs, or in `hud.gfx`. What JC3 does ship is **N projectiles per trigger aimed at one target**: `missile_per_shot` 6 (`w123`), `fire_amount` 15 (`w701` missile strike, into a 10 m radius), `bullets_per_shot` 8 (shotguns), and `burst_count` 6 fired sequentially (`w613`/`w614`). Those can build *N shots at one aim point*; they cannot build *N shots at N different targets*.

Labels: `decoded` = read at the archive entry and offset named; `derived` = arithmetic shown; `UNKNOWN` = searched in the named files and absent, with what would settle it. No game was launched. Everything below is regenerated by `python Tools/scripts/t_lock_salvo.py --write`.

### 4.1 `aim_timer_override` is the only candidate, and it is not 2.0

`decoded` - `WeaponTuning.aim_timer_override`, offset **108**, 4 B float. Distribution over the 74 `WeaponTuning` files: 8 file(s) at 4, 1 file(s) at 0.001, 65 file(s) at -1.

Every 4.0 is an RPG-class weapon (`w141`, `w141_mm110`, `w142`, `w143`, `w144`, `w146`, `w147`, `w704`) - guided and unguided alike - so the value tracks the two-handed rocket aim pose, not lock capability. The Stupka mortar is 0.001 and everything else is -1. **No tune carries 2.0 or 1.0**, and w143's own 4.0 cannot be BmAK's 2.0 s hold.

`UNKNOWN`: what the engine does with 4.0. It is named as an *aim timer override* and is the closest thing to a hold in the weapon data; a probe holding an RPG sight for 2 s vs 4 s would settle whether it gates anything. It does not provide BmAK's value.

### 5.1 Property-name hash test

`decoded` - the weapon objects carry 32-bit property ids. The Jenkins function used by `jc3wiring.py` resolves the known names tuning_file=AA7096C9, autoaim_tuning_file=6D4C8277, main_weapon=851CAABE and was then run over **2,625,640** one-to-four-word candidate names containing lock/hold/time/target/salvo/volley/count. **Matches: none.**

`derived` - three distinct mechanisms, all single-target:

1. **`missile_per_shot` 6 on `w123`** (UPU-210 grenade launcher): six projectiles leave on one trigger pull, from one weapon, aimed by the one `AutoAim` target the weapon holds. The field is a count of projectiles, not of targets.

The 15/10 block is also present on the balloon, namaste, sungun, typhoon and EMP tunes (a shared default), but only `w701` is a `CMissileStrikeWeapon`.

Muzzles do not multiply targets: `w618` has 4 fire points and `w501` 6, yet both have `missile_per_shot` 1. `SWeaponFirePattern` chooses which muzzle fires next; it has no target field. **N shots at one target is buildable from data; N shots at N targets is not**: JC3's `AutoAim` resolves one current target per weapon, and no tune, entity or HUD record holds a list of N targets.

## 10. Verdict and what can be built

* **2.0 s hold:** no member. The nearest is `aim_timer_override` 4.0, which is an RPG-class aim timer, not 2.0 and not lock-gated. The engine's lock progression is native: the HUD only consumes a 0-1 value (`UC_SetLockonValue`) or paints a POI. To get 2.0 s, BmAK's timer has to stay native, or a script must gate fire for 2.0 s after acquisition.
* **8-target salvo:** no member. N projectiles exist (6, 15, 8) but all resolve to one aim point. A true 8-target salvo needs a bridge: for each of 8 acquired targets, set the weapon's aim/target and fire one projectile; the tune data cannot express it.
* **Buildable now from data:** a 6-shot volley at one target (`w123` `missile_per_shot` 6), a 6-shot sequential burst (`w613`/`w614`), a 15-missile 10 m strike (`w701`), or an 8-pellet shotgun spread. Each is one target/area, not eight.

### UNKNOWNs

1. **What `aim_timer_override` 4.0 gates.** *Tried:* every tune's value, the RPG set, the entity props. *Would settle:* holding an RPG sight for 2 s vs 4 s, or native code.
2. **`id:098D3AA3` (0.2) and `id:D13BBA30` (0.25)** on `w618`/`w123`. *Tried:* the Jenkins vocabulary in 5.1. *Would settle:* the engine's name table / native code.
3. **Whether `UC_SetLockonValue` is fed by weapon lock or vehicle tracking.** It is named `Tracking.VehicleLockon*` and lives in the radar module, so it is most likely the vehicle tracker; the weapon POI path is separate. *Would settle:* a HUD trace.
4. **The AutoAim angle unit** (turns vs radians), unchanged from `T_WP3B_LOCKON.md`.
5. **Whether the engine can hold several targets per weapon.** No data member names a list; `AutoAim` exposes one current target. *Would settle:* native disassembly or a probe.
