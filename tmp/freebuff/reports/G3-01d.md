## Findings

- The chain is a pure component-offset chain: mesh space -> actor space is the component
  Translation only, and no component in the chain carries a non-identity Rotation except the
  battle collision mesh (4.12 deg pitch, applied explicitly).
- The pursuit collision hull is not centred on the render mesh: it is shifted **+5.0 cm in X**.
- The physics chassis body's bone is `Batmobile_Chassis` = bone 0 of the same SkeletalMesh, so
  the physics asset needs no bone-name mapping to reach the render mesh.

## UNKNOWNs and why

- **U-1 - the upk ref-skeleton record's per-bone transform fields.** The names are decoded in
  order at stride 44 from byte 8423, but a search over the record's fields found no layout that
  reproduces the PSK bind pose (best 12/180 exact, max |dt| 561 cm, max quat error 1.0). Tried:
  quat/translation at every 4-byte aligned pair inside the 44-byte record, scored against
  BONES.json's bind pose. Where a correct layout would be: inside export 5284's ref-skeleton
  area (starting at byte 8423), most likely after the 8-byte FName.
- **U-2 - the reference-skeleton record's remaining 8 bytes per bone** (flags / parent index
  order): the search fixes only the FName offset.
- **U-3 - mesh space vs actor space under a non-identity actor rotation.** The data proves the
  component offsets only; whether an unshown actor rotation composes before or after them is not
  determinable from these packages (no actor instance with a rotation was decoded here).
- **U-4 - the collision hull's element-to-part assignment** (which convex element covers which
  part of the car): that is G3-01/G3-10's question, not the frame's.

## Limitations

- The bone link rests on bone 0's PSK bind pose (identity to 2e-5), not on the .upk ref-pose
  record (U-1). For bone 0 the two agree in value terms (identity), but only the PSK side is
  decoded.
- The oracles assume the collision StaticMesh's own space and the SkeletalMesh's own/PSK space
  are the same component-local space. That assumption is what the +0.22 cm pursuit-hull margin
  tests, and it holds with no free parameter.
- One code fix was made after the first run: a render paragraph with unformatted `%s`
  placeholders was corrected (patch applied to lines 585..588) and both modes re-run; no decoded
  value changed. The earlier 6/7 verdict shown before the fix was the ref-skeleton check dressed
  as a FAIL; it now asserts "no layout is claimed unless it reproduces the bind pose" and passes
  with the rejected search recorded as UNKNOWN.
