Pursuit <-> battle mode at runtime (RB-4; decision D-2 reopened). Operator facts:
- LT hold transforms the car in about 2 s: the turret rises from the body, and the wheels and body reconfigure.
- The swap brings a near-instant slowdown.
- The tracker HUD persists across the switch.
- Armour is identical in both modes.

First read the P9 result in reports/l0-rc4-probe-session.md: did the console spawn our entity, and does it take a path, a name or a hash?

Then write the prototype in Tools/hook/. On the LT-hold event:
1. Record position, rotation, velocity and health.
2. Spawn the battle entity through the spawn system (addresses in console-thingy's spawn_system.h).
3. Seat the player as the driver and carry the health over.
4. Apply the lore slowdown.
5. Remove the pursuit entity.

Do the reverse on release. If seating has no known function, find it (console-thingy sources, RTTI vtables) or mark it UNKNOWN with the experiment that would settle it.
