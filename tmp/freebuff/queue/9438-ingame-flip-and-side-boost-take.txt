In-game take for self-righting and side boosters using cards C3_FLIP.md and C4_SIDEBOOST.md in Working/Q/PILOT/. Operator facts: the flip thrusters fire only after the car has rested on its roof or side for a while, and left stick plus A fires the side boosters. Flip: drive off a ramp so the car lands on its roof, then on its side, then stay completely idle; start the stopwatch at the first telemetry sample where speed is zero. Observation: the time to the first rotation sample and whether the car ends upright. Pass rule: the car ends on its wheels, and the delay is the value chosen by brief 9124 plus or minus 0.5 s, and it does not right itself before that delay. Side boost: at 60 km/h press stick left with A held; lateral displacement is read from telemetry. Pass rule: the lateral impulse matches the spec of brief 7964. Also drive into deep water once and confirm the teleport to land. Fail route: lines in Working/Q/FIXES.md naming the idle timer cell, thruster force or the water graph. Files in Working/Q/caps/c3/.
